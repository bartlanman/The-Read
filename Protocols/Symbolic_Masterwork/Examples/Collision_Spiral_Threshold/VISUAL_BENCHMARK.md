# VISUAL BENCHMARK — SPIRAL × THRESHOLD

**Status:** User-preferred design direction, not final production asset or proven cultural sign.  
**Work:** *The Altered Horizon* (Spiral × Threshold).  
**Decision context:** Initial geometric concept → ornate alchemical/cosmological rendering → minimalist monotone rendering → user response: **“i like it.”**

## Preferred visual direction

The selected conversation rendering is:
- **minimalist** with **one dark ink tone on a light field**;
- a bold continuous spiral with an open outer end;
- a straight vertical axis crossing or interrupting the spiral's visual field;
- balanced negative space and no celestial, botanical, architectural or allegorical ornament;
- relatively thick, clean strokes, scalable in visual character.

The preference is for the **specific minimalist rendering shown in the conversation**, not retroactive approval of the older [ALTERED_HORIZON.svg](ALTERED_HORIZON.svg). The existing SVG is retained as an earlier historical sketch.

**Asset status:** The exact user-approved raster image is retained in the conversation history but **has not been copied into this repository by this record**. Do not substitute an approximately redrawn SVG and claim it is the approved image. When the original image bytes can be transferred, add them as a separate versioned asset and link the commit here; prepare an editable vector only after visual comparison/approval.

## What is and isn't affirmed

**Affirmed:** minimalist, monochromatic visual direction; preference over both the line-and-spiral rough sketch and the ornate rendering.

**Not affirmed:** a final logo, automatic transformation of meaning at a crossing, a universal interpretation of spiral or threshold, or adoption by a community.

## Remaining design inquiry

The visual still resembles spiral + vertical line. Can an equally restrained geometry produce a change in viewer perception **through** the crossing, rather than merely narrating the idea afterward? Any new candidate should compete with this benchmark; do not overwrite it.
