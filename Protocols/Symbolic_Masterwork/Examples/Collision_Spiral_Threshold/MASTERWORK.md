# COLLISION FIELD 001 — SPIRAL × THRESHOLD

**Status:** Candidate symbolic Masterwork · 2026-11-07  
**Source records:** [Spiral Read](../Spiral/SYMBOL_READ.md) · [Threshold Read](../Threshold/SYMBOL_READ.md).  
**This is a creative encounter, not historical proof of a merged ancestral symbol.**

## Independent propositions

**Spiral:** a turning path of changed radial relation. *Proposed symbolic capacity:* continuity through changed relation.

**Threshold:** a distinguishable boundary and possibility of passage. *Proposed symbolic capacity:* passage across a consequential distinction.

Neither idea entails the other. A spiral may turn without crossing a boundary; a threshold may be crossed without a changed capacity.

## Collision

If the spiral's changing position is interrupted by a threshold, a question appears: does crossing merely change location, or change **what can next become perceptible**?

**New candidate proposition:** *A passage can change the field of subsequent possible recognitions.*

This is not automatically earned by placing a door over a spiral. Its conceptual distinction is that the passage alters **future orientation**, not merely present location. It remains a proposition until an interpretation or practice makes it meaningful to participants.

## Contemporary Masterwork — design proposal

**Working name:** *The Altered Horizon*.

The visual grammar should place an expanding continuous path on both sides of a clear boundary, but the path's relative spacing or orientation changes after crossing. Retain continuity of the line and the perceptibility of the distinction; avoid literal quotations of a sacred gate, triple spiral, or inherited religious motif.

**Original sketch:** [SVG](ALTERED_HORIZON.svg). It is a conceptual formal study, not a final adopted cultural mark.

**Image operations:** discontinuous access; continuous path; phase shift in relationship after crossing; open outside.

**Candidate motto:** *A crossing changes what can be seen.*

## Alternative outcomes and refusals

- **Spiral inside doorway:** rejected as additive collage rather than a relational collision.
- **Concentric circle + portal:** rejected because no changing radial relation would remain.
- **Compulsory upward arrow:** rejected because progress or moral improvement is not historically or logically established.
- **Threshold as guaranteed transformation:** refused. Crossings need not change a participant's capacity.
