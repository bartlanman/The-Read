# CONTEMPORARY MASTERWORK 003 — THE BETWEEN IMAGE

**Status:** first minimalist monochrome candidate, not approved or culturally adopted.  
**Symbol:** [Vector construction](BETWEEN_IMAGE.svg) — an independent geometric study based on mirrored crescents.  
**Rendered conversation study:** a generated monochrome emblem shown in the conversation; exact raster has not been committed, and the vector is an interpretation rather than a pixel-identical reproduction.

## Visual question
Can two apparently opposing forms create a third image that exists only because of the relation between them?

## Proposed fundamental
**Presence through difference.** Reflection makes an appearance present without making it identical to its source. This does not entail that a mirror tells the truth or that symmetry equals identity.

## Three design approaches (Visual Genesis Protocol)
1. **Twin crescents / negative-space apparition:** two facing contours generate a third interior shape. **Selected for first experiment**; strongest small-scale and monochrome potential.
2. **Broken symmetry:** reflected contour differs subtly from the original; suggests the instability or selection of viewpoints. **Reserved**; risks reading as error rather than relation.
3. **Reversible figure-ground:** centered shape can be read alternately as positive form or void. **Reserved**; strongest perceptual experiment but could compromise emblem legibility.

## Design rationale
The central white form is not drawn as a separate positive object. It is created by the encounter of the opposing black shapes, and so exists as a perceived third term. A thin central spine makes the reflective axis legible. No Hathor emblem, Chinese cosmogram or historical sacred mark is copied.

## Integrity audit
**Achieved:** monochrome restraint, visible relational dependency and memorable silhouette.  
**Not achieved:** documented viewer recognition of the intended concept; historical evidence of this mark; proof of an emergent cultural meaning.  
**Observed cultural response:** none recorded.  
**Status:** candidate; awaiting user critique.

## Motto (proposed)
*The image is not the thing.*
