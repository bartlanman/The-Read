## Atlas relations
**Resonance:** mirror reflects appearance in multiple media and places.  
**Difference:** an Egyptian burial artifact, Chinese inscribed bronze, and European allegorical mirror are not the same symbolic system.  
**Opposition:** revealing versus deceiving; self-knowledge versus vanity.  
**Candidate fundamental:** an image of something made present at a remove from its source.  
**Remainder:** this is a contemporary formal inference, not a cross-cultural doctrine.
