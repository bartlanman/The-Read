## Tensions preserved
- **Transfer vs activation:** the flame is carried, but an electrical relay triggers another circuit.
- **Continuity vs sameness:** the operation may continue in modified form.
- **Cooperation vs instrumental replacement:** historical hunting and labor uses need not imply ethical solidarity.
- **Ancient vs modern:** Olympic torch relay has modern origins despite antique precedents.

## Relational candidate
*Continuity through transferred agency* is a creative proposition supported by a comparative analogy across cases, not a universal historical belief. In design, the new visual question is how a continuous field is produced through a moment of distinct participation.
