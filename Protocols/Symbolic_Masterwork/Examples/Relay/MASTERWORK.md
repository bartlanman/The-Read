# CONTEMPORARY MASTERWORK 004 — RELAY

**Status:** Creative candidate, not user-approved or culturally adopted.  
**Proposed fundamental:** **continuity through transferred agency**.  
**Sources:** [Symbol Read](SYMBOL_READ.md) and [Atlas](ATLAS_AND_ENCOUNTERS.md).

## Visual question
Can a single continuity be experienced as depending on two distinct carriers, with its identity located in the handoff rather than exclusively in either carrier?

## Three visual operations
1. **Interlocking arcs (first image experiment):** two unequally sized, distinct curved paths almost touch at a pinch. A fine axis runs through their meeting. The visible moment of transfer is most salient. Concern: this could be mistaken for an infinity symbol, merely suggest perpetual motion, or fail to show distinct agency.
2. **Alternating positive/negative path:** one form resolves into negative space carried by another; the same interval continues across changed material. Concern: optical complexity at small sizes.
3. **Successive interruption/restart:** a continuous line is impossible until two separated units align, making a passage possible. Concern: may overstate harmony or present one obligatory direction.

## Visual direction
Minimalist, monochromatic, bold, restrained, legible without a caption; compatible with the user's preferred Spiral × Threshold and Mirror expressions but not identical to either. Avoid arrows, batons, electrical wiring symbols, sacred borrowing and gratuitous adornment.

## Concept generated in conversation
First candidate is a raster study of two interlocking dark arcs and a narrow axial transfer, on light cream. The exact generated image is **not in the GitHub repo**; do not claim an approximate vector recreation to be the original. Preserve it as a conversation image pending user decision and asset transfer.

## Candidate identity
**Working title:** *The Handoff*  
**Motto:** *What continues does not belong to one carrier.*

## Evaluation
**Formal clarity:** two differentiated halves and a perceptible point of near transfer.  
**Conceptual tension:** continuity depends on another carrier, but not every baton/circuit analogue is interchangeable.  
**Possible failure:** a viewer might see two circular waves or an infinity form, not a relay.  
**Cultural status:** proposed. No independent encounters, recognition or adoption recorded.
