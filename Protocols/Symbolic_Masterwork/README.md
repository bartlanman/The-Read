## Working Symbol Index

The initial [searchable Symbol Index](Index/README.md) catalogs Spiral, Threshold, and their first collision with source links, typed propositions and relationships. Use the [JSON catalog](Index/symbols.json) and [Python search tool](Index/search_index.py) from a local checkout; local queries create retrieval logs, while historical encounter ledgers remain separately reviewed. The six reference collections are named lenses, not imported datasets.

## Run it

Say **`Symbol Read: Spiral`** to research a symbol from earliest recoverable evidence through its contemporary lives and articulate its *fundamental form, human encounter, and proposed relational capacity*.

Say **`Symbolic Masterwork: Spiral + Threshold`** to complete the research, cross-collection/tradition Atlas, relational collision, and original contemporary symbol concepts.

Say **`Revisit Symbol: [ID] under [new context]`** to develop the meaning record without losing the previous one.

**Always:** record an Encounter each time you consult the Index or an external symbol resource. All six reference collections receive a relevance/access status, not invented coverage. Retain the difference among sourced history, source interpretation, our interpretation, creative proposal, and observed cultural use.

## Reference field

The six recurring lenses are **ARAS / The Book of Symbols; Warburg / Mnemosyne Atlas; Cirlot / A Dictionary of Symbols; Chevalier & Gheerbrant / Dictionary of Symbols; Iconclass; Getty emblem and iconography resources**.

Relevant symbolic traditions may include Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Greek/Roman, alchemical/Hermetic, Hindu, Buddhist, Chinese cosmological, Jewish/Kabbalistic, Christian, Islamic, specific Indigenous, mathematical and contemporary systems. Treat each in its context. The collection list is a **coverage audit**, not a claim that each resource is accessible or relevant every time.

## Minimum deliverable

A full Masterwork provides (1) sourced **Symbol Read**, (2) comparative **Atlas**, (3) original contemporary design with a traceable rationale, and (4) a **Living Symbol Record**. If evidence or rights are insufficient, log the limitation and HOLD the affected stage instead of asserting completion.

## Repository scope

This folder initially supplies the **protocol and reusable templates**. It does **not** claim the six collections have been imported, that a searchable index or generator has been implemented, or that any example symbol has already passed a full Read. Future index entries and artwork should be added as separate sourced records.

**Terminal distinction:** A symbol can be designed; cultural significance cannot be certified by its designer.
