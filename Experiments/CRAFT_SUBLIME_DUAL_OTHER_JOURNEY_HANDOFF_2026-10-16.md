## 4. Character source B — SUBLIME

**Earned source identity:** Meaningfully encountered excess: an encounter in which something significant remains present while exceeding the recipient's customary capacity to encompass it.

May condition local situations featuring a limit of apprehension, disproportion between what appears and how it is received, significant experience that resists familiar measure, and variable response.

**Crucial asymmetry:** The sublime is an encounter relation, **not a supernatural property owned by a person**. The character may be situated in, mediate, witness, resist, or participate in such relations. They may also fail to encounter any sublimity in a given event.

**Source refusals:** Sublime does not necessarily mean large, terrifying, beautiful, divine, pleasant, good, elevated, or transformative. Overwhelm alone does not prove meaning or growth. The label **B / SUBLIME** belongs to preparation, not the character's knowledge, identity, or dialogue.
