# THERE — Historical conditions

Research antecedent conditions without assuming they inevitably produced alchemy.

## Lines of inquiry
- Metallurgy, alloying, gilding, pigments, dyes, glass, mineral processing, and craft experimentation.
- Workshops, division of labor, apprenticeship, and circulation of secret or public recipes.
- Temple institutions, religious objects, sacred materials, and claims to revelation.
- Greek theories of matter; Egyptian technical and religious traditions.
- Imperial administration, patrons, markets, taxation, and changing access to knowledge.
- Language, writing, copying, scholarly authority, and the use of pseudonymous authors.

For each claim, record *place, era, evidence, and what it warrants*. Distinguish craft genealogy from textual genealogy.
