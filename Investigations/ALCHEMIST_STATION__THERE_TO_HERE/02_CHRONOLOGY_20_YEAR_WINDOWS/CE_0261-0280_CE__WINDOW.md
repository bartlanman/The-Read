# Window 24 — 261–280 CE: The Mint Rewrites Sovereignty; a Scholar's Papyrus Becomes a Will

**Status:** TEN-LAYER SOURCE-CRITICAL DEEP PASS (2026-10-18) — documentary and numismatic original-object anchors independently checked. **NO SECURE ALCHYMIST / ALCHEMIST PRACTITIONER IDENTIFIED IN 261–280 CE**. **Grade:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED DEEP DETAIL, not crossing-verified creation of an alchemical occupation. **Geography:** Roman Egypt, especially Alexandria and Oxyrhynchus, with Palmyra/Antioch as related political context. China retains independent comparative lineage, with no evidenced Greek–Chinese technical transfer in this window.

### Research question

**What becomes of a station when the outside world changes whom a material object is permitted to name, and when a literary manuscript acquires a new practical life?** These instances are **comparators**, NOT evidence that Aurelian, Zenobia, mint-workers or a Roman testamentary official were Alchemists. They permit the investigation to specify what a **physical survival, public authority inscription and material/knowledge technique** each do and do not prove.

## 01 — THERE: surrounding conditions with date precision

- **260/after cross-window warning:** Gallienus's restoration of Christian worship rights, as quoted in Eusebius *Ecclesiastical History* VII.13, probably belongs around the aftermath of Valerian's **260** capture; its precise issue year **may overlap this window** but is NOT newly assigned as a 261 event. This is a clergy station response, not alchemical restoration. [Window 23 T04]
- **263/264 original mint witness:** Ashmolean **HCR31547**, Alexandrian billon tetradrachm of **Gallienus**, with Greek imperial title and `L ΙΑ` (his **year eleven**) plus military imagery of Ares/soldier with captive(s). Material and year mark demonstrate an operational mint and publicly named regime, not identification of the maker. [W24-O00]
- **c.268–270:** Gallienus is assassinated **268**, succeeded by Claudius II; Aurelian acclaimed **270**. General political context comes from historical syntheses, but do not assign an uncataloged local manufacturing change to one day.
- **270/271:** surviving Alexandria **billon coin HCR31960 / RPC X 75751** presents **Aurelian** on obverse, **Vaballathus** on reverse. Their honors are not equal. This **dual naming** evidences a concrete political accommodation/claim imprinted by a mint; it is not proof of peaceful agreement among their territories or a fusion ritual. [W24-O01]
- **272:** **Zenobia** appears as **Augusta** in original Alexandrian year-five billon coin types (**RPC X 75758** and comparators), including a type with **Homonoia**. Authority rhetoric changed from named lesser ruler alongside Aurelian to Zenobia asserting a full imperial title. The exact chronology of each Antioch/Alexandrian issue is best reconstructed coin by coin; do not equate a mint image with uncontested sovereign legality. [W24-O02, S01–S02]
- **272–273:** Aurelian defeats Palmyrene forces and reasserts control; 273 subsequent revolt at Palmyra and consequences are known from historical synthesis and archaeological discussion. **Do not** use the conflict as proof that alchemical books or workshops were targeted. [W24-S02]
- **273/274:** **RPC X 75719**, a dated Aurelian **year five** Alexandrian debased-silver issue, puts Aurelian alone on the obverse with Zeus's head and star on reverse. Material mint competence and formal state name continue in at least the recorded type, regardless of individual workforce identities. [W24-O03]
- **c.274:** Aurelian's imperial monetary reform and marks used on new coinage are historical/numismatic themes. The meaning of certain marks (e.g. `XXI`/`KA`) is debated and must not be presented as an unquestioned recipe for silver-to-gold. [W24-S05]
- **276 — exact physical documentary anchor:** British Library **Papyrus 2040** preserves **two materially distinct uses on one object**. The earlier written recto is *Kestoi* Book 18 of identifiable author Sextus Julius Africanus, copied broadly **225–275/276**; a different scribe placed a **copy of the will of Hermogenes alias Eudaemon** on the reverse and this text is dated **276**. Thus a real reused manuscript survived changing authors, genres, owner/use environments and authority regimes without demonstrating a living Alchemist. The date of earlier copy is still unresolved. [W24-P01]
- **276–280:** no independent named **Alchemist** with a dated practical recipe, contemporary public certification and demonstrable performed transmutation is identified in the examined sample. Silence in our sample is NOT proof the profession did not exist.

## 02 — MATERIAL TECHNIQUE AND OPERATION

**Actual objects:**
1. **Gallienus HCR31547 (263/264):** billon tetradrachm, struck, c.**9.35 g**, **24.3 mm**. Minting involves formed metal and struck imagery; **we do not know the actual worker or exact composition formula**. [W24-O00]
2. **Aurelian/Vaballathus HCR31960 (270/271):** struck **billon tetradrachm**, **8.98 g**, **21.8 mm**. Two distinct ruler portraits, no named mint technician. [W24-O01]
3. **Zenobia RPC X 75758 (272):** **debased silver** type, ~**8.90 g average** cataloged, year five `L E`; a **specific altered specimen** HCR32033 appears with `L Δ` but its catalog reports later **tooling**: epsilon was erased and delta was cut. The **altered letter is not an original date on the die**, nor evidence of an independently minted year four Zenobia type. **Tooling date/motive unknown.** [W24-O02]
4. **Aurelian RPC X 75719 (273/274):** debased-silver coin type with his sole title, `L E`, Zeus/star; physical continuity of coin issuance does not prove continuity of individual artisans. [W24-O03]
5. **BL Papyrus 2040 (276 reuse):** recto original literary text written along fibers; later Greek legal **verso across fibers and upside down**; specifically an act of **material repurposing**, not a demonstrated coded ingredient substitution or recipe reconstitution. [W24-P01]

**Interpreting metal:** billon, alloying, debasement, mint striking, plating, gilding or imitative gold-like appearance are physical and economic techniques, **not validated elemental transmutation**. No performed laboratory gold-conversion assay or chemically analyzed product attached to named contemporary Alchemist. Later Leiden X/Stockholm recipes are a **BROADLY DATED FUTURE HORIZON**, not certain evidence of a 270 workshop. [W24-S04]

## 03 — LANGUAGE, ALLEGORY, SCRIBAL INVERSION AND LATER TOOLING

**Greek state names:** Gallienus's `ΑΥΤ Κ Π ΛΙΚ ΓΑΛΛΙΗΝΟϹ ϹΕΒ`; Aurelian's titled obverse (with `L A` for 270/271); the separately titled Vaballathus reverse; and **Zenobia `Ϲ Ε ΠΤΙΜ ΖΗΝΟΒΙΑ Ϲ Ε Β`** spell out competing claims about who occupies imperial authority. The word `Σεβαστή` / Augusta is an **office/rank claim**. Not an ancient word for trained goldmaker.

**Regnal `L IA`, `L E`:** public chronological numbers on coin types; not esoteric chemical ratios. **`Homonoia`**, the personified concord on a 272 Zenobia reverse, is **imperial-political representation**; a modern “unity” metaphor cannot turn it into an alchemical cell fusion diagram. Zeus, stars, eagles, gods on coins are public visual ideology, not ingredient cryptograms without positive evidence.

**The most precise provenance trap:** [RPC X 75758](https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/75758) catalogs **HCR32033** under a year-five Zenobia type and specifically reports **“rev. tooled, Ɛ erased and Δ cut in field to l.”** The [Ashmolean specimen page](https://hcr.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/coin/hcr32033) duly records the altered current surface as `L Δ` and labels the coin **tooled**. Thus a reader looking at only the *object as presently cut* could create a **false period event**. Must distinguish **original struck date**, **post-striking physical intervention**, **modern museum transcription**. The tooling's perpetrator and date are UNKNOWN.

**Africanus's `Kestoi`:** an author's early-third-century named work; the physically surviving **Book 18**, on Homeric textual authenticity, is not the extant location of the geometric/music recipe substitutions and lost appendix explored in Window 22. The **276 will** written on the back was a completely different genre, in Greek, to regulate inheritance; the existence of these two sides does not identify the earlier roll owner or an alchemical initiation. [W24-P01]

## 05 — PERCEPTION: MULTIPLE AUDIENCES, DIFFERENT TYPES OF WARRANT

- The **minting political claim** was visible on coins, but tells us **what officially issued objects asserted**, not what everyone in Egypt privately accepted.
- **Vaballathus** bearing titulature on Aurelian's reverse shows a politically loaded formal relationship rather than demonstrable equality. The subsequent **Zenobia Augusta** assertion is an issuer's public claim, not a later neutral historian's verdict.
- **Homonoia** iconography can express desired concord amid conflict; evidence of rhetorical representation **is not evidence of achieved concord**.
- **Hermogenes**' 276 will is evidence of a **councillor/officeholder's legal and property station**; its use of an older literary roll does not imply that its named testator studied, valued or repudiated *Kestoi*.
- Modern numismatic and papyrological readers have to separate **original object's content**, **the artifact's later alterations**, **museum dating**, and **modern reconstructive inference**; the tooled coin is a positive counterexample to reading present text as untouched original.
- Later **Zosimos/Maria** reception gives an alchemical authorial tradition and claimed remembered masters, but not a precisely located 261–280 member of such a community.

## 06 — THE STATION (WITHOUT THE UNWARRANTED ALCHEMIST)

**Empire and governor:** various minted names and titles correspond to public claims; precise local recognition in moments of war is historically contested, and an actual coin is not a universal opinion poll.

**Mint workers:** evident collectively from struck metal; personal skill likely, but **workers not identified** and not permitted to borrow the ruler's title.

**Legal actor Hermogenes:** explicitly identifiable **councillor and officeholder**, with will probated/copied 276. No document labels him an alchemist or says he authored the physical recto.

**Named historical technical author Africanus:** genuinely an earlier identifiable *Kestoi* writer; he is NOT demonstrably alive or practicing in 276. The survival of his earlier work is textual afterlife, not present-tense professional station.

**Maria and Zosimos:** tradition-bearing alchemical names, without secure precisely 261–280 age, employment, original manuscript or laboratory in the reviewed witnesses.

**Verdict: NO SECURE NAMED 261–280 ALCHEMIST IDENTIFIED.** This does **not** exclude undetected artisan workshops; it rejects a fabricated exact-life/titled station.

## 07 — LINEAGE, COPYING AND MATERIAL PROVENANCE

1. **Africanus Kestoi composition c.222–235 (work dedicated to Severus Alexander)** → **physical copy of Book 18 c.225–275/276 (recto of BL Papyrus 2040)** → **276 copy of Hermogenes's will on same sheet's verso** → papyrus later excavated/cataloged. **Consequence:** an earlier person's literary authority survives via an object's repurposing; NO attested student, recipe transmission, joint meeting or copy of the lost notation key. [W24-P01; W22]
2. **Gallienus coin 263/264** → later **270/271 dual Aurelian/Vaballathus** → **272 Zenobia Augusta** → **273/274 Aurelian alone**: a sequence of *distinct original physical objects*, **not** proof of a single workshop's staff, a teacher line or a new metallurgy discovery. [W24-O00–O03]
3. **Original Zenobia 272 issue `L E`** → undated **post-striking tooling** of one specimen `E` to `Δ` → modern Ashmolean transcription and RPC correction. This is an artifact-reception genealogy, **not** a new ancient minting event. [W24-O02]
4. **Leiden/Stockholm manuscript horizon and later Zosimos:** a *separate later manuscript and commentary tradition*. Neither derives from the coin issues or 276 will in any demonstrated lineage.
5. **Chinese independent field:** no original 261–280 evidence has established transmission to/from these Egyptian material or textual artifacts.

## 09 — HERE, FAILURE CONDITIONS AND REMAINDER

**Shown:** objects can carry formal public authority even when the emitting regime changes; the same material medium can be reused for a new socially authoritative act; original craft/authority, later textual or physical reuse, and present cataloged marks **must be disentangled**. A coin type's surviving modified specimen proves that **an apparent date can be an intervention after minting**, not a real additional ancient event. A papyrus verso establishes secure date **276** for a legal document without proving the recto's exact copying date.

**Does not show:** successful metal-to-gold conversion; an officially licensed or socially independently attested Alchemist; named mint-workers as religious experts; actual Aurelian attack on an alchemist guild; a certain Aurelian-era destruction of the Alexandrian Library (Suski disputes the usual simple claim); Africanus alive in 276; Hermogenes owned the book by himself; a lost appendix burned in the same war; original `L Δ` Zenobia issue from altered coin.

**Unknown:** exact materials in each alloy and changes through manufacturing; social response of workshops to political regime changes; when and why the surviving coin was tooled; why the literary roll was reused; whether its original owner is reconstructible; any actual contemporaneous goldmaking claim in an original artifact; prior generation of later dye-recipe papyri; Maria's historical date/role; specifics of Gallienus restitution timing.

**The most profound finding:** **A physical witness is not necessarily the untouched record of its first maker.** A coin can be struck under one station, recut later to say something chronologically different, and faithfully transcribed by a modern catalog as it appears now. A text can be copied from an older author then receive a legally authoritative will on its back. **Source interpretation must recover stages of material change before inferring what a historical station knew, claimed or permitted.** Unlike earlier abstract caveats about mysterious texts, **we can see and document the material alteration itself**.
