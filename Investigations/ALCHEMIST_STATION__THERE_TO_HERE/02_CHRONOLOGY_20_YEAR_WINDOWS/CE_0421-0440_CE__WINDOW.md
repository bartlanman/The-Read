# Window 32 — 421–440 CE: The State Authorizes Teachers, Editors and the Destruction of Certain Books

**Status:** TEN-LAYER SOURCE-CRITICAL DEEP PASS (2026-10-18). **Central result:** **state authority over PUBLIC TEACHING (425), EDITING/CANONIZING LAW (429–439), and the explicit suppression of NAMED THEOLOGICAL books (435)** is much more securely evidenced than any alchemical institution in this period. **NO SECURE 421–440 ALCHEMIST, CHRYSOPOEIA TEST OR ALCHEMY-SPECIFIC CENSORSHIP.**

## 01 — THERE — real authorities and chronology

**423:** CTh XVI.10.22–24 mandates penalties for pagan sacrifice yet warns against attacks on peaceful Jews/pagans. **27 Feb 425:** CTh XIV.9.3 authorizes public teachers and differentiates private instruction. **26 March 429** and **20 Dec 435:** CTh I.1.5–6 establish/revise a formal imperial law-editing commission. **3 Aug 435:** CTh XVI.5.66 orders Nestorius-attributed writings sought and burned. **Feb 438:** Theodosian Code promulgated; **25 Dec 438** Roman senate reception; effective **1 Jan 439**. **Socrates** composes history ~439–440, narrating Hypatia murdered **415**. No 421–440 alchemist securely identifiable.

## 02 — MATERIAL TECHNIQUE — standard objects versus synthetic gold

**Original 425–430 exagium solidi**, BM 1987,0703.24, shows official **coin-weight standard**, with marked purpose and scales. A separate Theodosius II **gold solidus**, Berlin 18213856, ~424–430 catalog variants (4.37 g), and Alexandria **copper-alloy coin** BM 1995,0201.83, **425–435**, document real existing-metal fabrication. **No proven chemical conversion of copper into gold**, ore-to-mint process or named Alchemist; weight standard is not an assay of a newly synthesized element.

## 03 — LANGUAGE AND ALLEGORY — names that do different work

**425** `philosophiae arcana` refers to advanced/philosophical teaching within recognized chairs, not occult goldmaking. `usurpantes sibi nomina magistrorum` addresses unappointed public schoolteachers. **435** `demendi supervacanea verba et adiciendi necessaria et demutandi ambigua et emendandi incongrua` expressly authorizes changes in inherited legal wording—official editing, not accidental manuscript contamination or secret cipher. **435** `diligenti studio requiri ac publice conburi` directs searches and burning of **Nestorian theological books**, not chemistry. `EXAGIVM SOLIDI` labels a coin-weight standard, not gold created from a base metal.

## 05 — PERCEPTION — whose claim is recognized

The government **represented** pedagogical specialists as worthy of regulated public posts; an official text distinguishes approved public teachers from unauthorized public claimants. Imperial lawmakers presented their own selected and revised texts as legally binding. **435** branded writings of Nestorius unacceptable and ordered their destruction. These are **administrative claims/authorizations** rather than independent demonstrations that teachers were competent, editors preserved every original nuance, or manuscripts were universally burned. Early-fifth-century coin-weight iconography frames material weighing as official standard.

## 06 — STATION — teacher, compiler, censor, metalworker

**Recognized:** approved **schoolteacher**, compiler/court legal official, prosecutor of heresy, monetary issuer and coin-standard custodian. **Claim contested:** Nestorian writer labeled condemned; unauthorized public teacher claimed title improperly. **Socrates** is identifiable church historian retrospectively describing a philosopher. **Not recognized:** an explicitly licensed `alchemist` or identified 430 expert claiming ability to create elemental gold. The 31 prescribed chair slots (10 Latin grammarians, 3 Latin orators, 10 Greek grammarians, 5 Greek sophists, 1 philosophy, 2 law) cannot be read as actual filled appointments.

## 07 — LINEAGE — textual and individual transmission

**429 first compilation order → 435 revised editorial instructions → February 438 authoritative Code → 25 December 438 Roman Senate proceedings → January 439 entry into force → surviving early medieval/fifth–sixth-century manuscripts.** This is a **documented institutional selection/republication chain**, not a lineage of alchemical authors. **Socrates's history written c.439/440** transmits **415** Hypatia narrative. Hypatia's students do not become historical alchemists by later repeated references. **Pseudo-Synesius/Dioscorus** and **Olympiodorus the Alchemist** original attribution dates remain disputed, and no original 425–440 alchemical teaching contract is identified. Chinese and Greek lineages remain separate.

## 09 — HERE AND REMAINDER — actual change, unknowns

**HERE:** legally recognized public learning and binding text custody become highly structured. A **named state editing commission** is allowed to alter inherited legal words. A targeted **theological** book-burning order demonstrates that censorship can be legally explicit without establishing an indiscriminate scientific purge. **The exagium** witnesses authorized weighing, not material transformation. **NOT SHOWN:** a named practicing Alchemist, official chemical teacher chair, gold transmutation, 435 alchemical censorship, or alchemical connection to Hypatia. **UNKNOWN:** exact teacher rosters, actual books found and destroyed, the editorial changes applied to each original law, coin-weight use, and authentic chronology of Greek alchemical commentaries. **Most profound:** the **authority to choose and revise which texts remain authoritative** becomes an independently sanctioned institutional station; preservation is neither neutral copying nor evidence the older author personally held the new office.
