# The Alchemist — 20-year stack 200 BCE–2040 CE

**Status: OPEN, PROVISIONAL, SOURCE-ANCHORED CHRONOLOGICAL RESEARCH. Window 25 (281–300 CE) now deep-researched; Window 26 (301–320 CE) next; ST-001–ST-085.** Includes **112** contiguous 20-year windows; the final window **2021–2040 CE is current and incomplete (observed only through 2026-10-18).**

Core method: [10-layer Outside pressure audit](../00_METHOD_AND_EVIDENCE/OUTSIDE_PRESSURE_AUDIT.md) | [Original nine-layer protocol](../00_METHOD_AND_EVIDENCE/STACK_FIELD_PROTOCOL.md) | [Outside dossier](../10_OUTSIDE_PRESSURES/README.md)

There are two different evidentiary stages:
- **200 BCE–1700 CE: 95 inherited window files**, mostly first-pass research scaffolds (two expanded dossiers), now governed by the added pressure audit as a **pending retroactive test**. The old files do not each contain fully researched outside-pressure findings.
- **1701–2040 CE: 17 new twentieth-year windows**, including the current partially observed window and selected source-backed pressures. None represents a complete close-reading of all relevant primary texts.

**The later label pseudoscience must never be projected into ancient people's own vocabulary.** A broadly dated trend is not evidence that a social transition occurred exactly inside every 20-year interval.

## Our most important historical crossing
The old professional station is not always overthrown because the *operation* fails. The standards of public warrant, experimentation, language, patronage, publication, institutional membership and professional identity can alter the legitimacy of that work. Some material procedures survive under newer names, and some claims lose acceptance because the evidence fails.

## Boundary discipline
- **Not a proven automatic sequence:** alchemy → pseudoscience → chemistry is a caricature.
- **Not a gold-making vindication:** nuclear elemental transmutation is physically real, but not chemical chrysopoeia.
- **Not a completed corpus:** every broad date, undefined actor and missing source must remain open.
