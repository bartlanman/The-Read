# Transmission and reception

Trace **actual passage**, not a decorative sequence of cultures.

## Tracks
1. Greco-Egyptian composition and early reading communities.
2. Greek late-antique / Byzantine interpretation and manuscript preservation.
3. Syriac and Arabic mediation where evidenced.
4. Arabic authorship, reorganization, disputation, and new composition.
5. Medieval Latin translation and transformation of intellectual status.
6. Renaissance / early modern uses, including experimentation, medicine, and natural philosophy.
7. Modern historical and symbolic reinterpretations — always distinguish these from ancient self-descriptions.

Maintain parallel-tradition entries for Chinese and Indian alchemical histories where relevant; do not assert direct transmission without evidence.

For each claimed movement: **source text, target text, mechanism, date range, medium, witness, and what changed**.
