# Texts, manifestations, and manuscripts

**Question:** What texts manifested from the station, and what texts helped construct that station?

For every work record:
- Conventional and original-language title.
- Claimed author vs historically supported author.
- Date range of composition vs date of earliest extant copy.
- Language(s), versions, translation chain, citations and quotations.
- Intended audience, patronage, genre, technical claims, authority claims.
- Textual relationship to earlier works: source, commentary, adaptation, attribution, or independent development.
- Manuscript inventory identifiers, editions, and scholarly uncertainties.
- Historical consequence for the station, clearly distinguishing documented reception from our interpretation.

## Candidate corpora
*Four Books* attributed to Democritus; *Letter of Isis to Horus*; writings attributed to Maria; Zosimos's works; Greek alchemical compilations; Arabic works and the Emerald Tablet tradition; medieval Latin translations. The list is a queue for verification, not a settled chain.
