# ST-089 — 13 June 313 CE: Licinius' Posted Circular, Worship Rights and Restitution

**Scope:** comparative institutional policy, not creation of an Alchemist credential.  
**Date/event:** Constantine and Licinius meet at Milan early **313**; **Licinius's religious freedom and restitution letter** reportedly **posted 13 June 313 at Nicomedia**.  
**Witnesses:** Lactantius *DMP 48* Latin; Eusebius *HE X.5* Greek transmission, in later manuscripts; not a surviving original Milan edict inscription.  
**Evidence verdict:** **NEAR-CONTEMPORARY QUOTED ROMAN DIRECTIVE**; date/wording as transmitted; no link to named goldmaking specialists.  
**Window:** [26, 301–320 CE](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0301-0320_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
The 311 Galerius measure had partially reauthorized Christian gatherings after nearly a decade of persecution. Christian congregations and property had undergone seizure/loss and varying local restrictions.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The 313 letter grants people more general freedom to practice religion and directs **restitution of Christian meeting places and property** without charging the recipients; an administrative reversal acts on buildings, resources and legal access, not materials used for transmutation.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Imperial policy speaks to public peace and worship choice, while Lactantius/Eusebius frame it within divine justice. A state claiming religious tolerance is not claiming it validated any particular technical or philosophical operation of Christians.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
**Licinius**, after a Milan agreement with Constantine, directs provincial officials; Christian property associations are treated as legal actors capable of recovering assets. **The so-called “Edict of Milan” is a modern catch-all** for settlement/documentary steps; the texts available are not original signed 313 Milan stone documents.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Church assembly/property restriction largely reversed in the transmitted letter. **No evidenced Alchemist teacher appointment, secret-cipher license, suppression/release of goldmaking books, workshop entry authorization or proof of a Christian alchemical specialist's survival.**

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
Lactantius *DMP 48* Latin and Eusebius *HE X.5* Greek preserve versions of policy: **freedom to follow religious preference** and return of buildings/other possessions. Because Eusebius translates/forms a version, the exact two versions are not a single excavated original document. Do not misdate 313 posted Nicomedia letter to 311 or characterize it as a physical original from Milan.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
**313 post-Galerius political settlement** modifies legal standing and property access for Christians. It is precisely linked to a church institution and its meeting places, not craft guilds.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
The state orders restitution; local execution, particular building claims and restitution outcomes require individual records. No demonstrated change in an alchemical community's self-description or working methods.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[Lactantius DMP 48 and sourcebook framing](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/source/edict-milan.asp); [Eusebius HE X.5](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250110.htm). Original 313 inscribed posted letter not examined.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
Material resources, meeting places, and legal authorization can be **returned by formal order** to a previously repressed named station. There is a documented process of social restitution and institutional reauthorization—**without a chemical expert's new standing**.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Which individual properties returned exactly when, revisions to texts during manuscript transmission, implementation in different provinces, any named Greek alchemical worker connected to those rights or direct archaeological restoration of specific manuscripts.
