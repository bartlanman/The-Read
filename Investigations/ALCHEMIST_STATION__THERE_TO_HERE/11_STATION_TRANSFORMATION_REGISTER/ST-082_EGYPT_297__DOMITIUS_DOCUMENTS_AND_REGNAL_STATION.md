# ST-082 — 297 CE: Original Egyptian Documents Recognize a Rival Emperor

**Scope:** Roman Egyptian public/legal station comparator, NOT a documented Alchemist. **Event/witness date:** 297 CE, original-period Greek papyri (specialist edition/catalog indexes). **Evidence verdict:** ATTESTED regnal usage; CONTESTED chronology and relation to Achilleus; NO SECURE EVIDENCE chemical expertise. **Window:** [25, 281–300](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0281-0300_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Diocletian's tetrarchic regime governed Roman Egypt and managed currency, tax and administrative office. The late-290s revolt made imperial recognition contested.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
Egyptian scribes dated petitions, petitions and leases using **Lucius Domitius Domitianus** as Augustus; **P.Isid.139**, **P.Isid.99**, **P.Isid.62**, **P.Thead.26**, and **P.Michael.24** provide independently published *documentary acts*. No chemical process occurs in their identified content. The **2 December 297** Tebtynis lease **P.Michael.24** concerns **arable land/rents/obligations**, NOT metals.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Writers used the claimant's regnal formula for legal timekeeping. That demonstrates **administrative usage** by document makers and readers, not universal agreement or acknowledged chemistry expertise. The social act of naming a living emperor differs from a sixth-century writer naming an ancient rebel.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Domitianus claimed Augustus status; Roman tetrarchic legitimacy was contested. The [*Prosopography of the Later Roman Empire* Domitianus entry](https://connectinglateantiquities.org/text.html?id=plre-1-domitianus-6) lists original papyri spanning late August through early December 297. **Aurelius Achilleus** is named by later literary chroniclers as leader and in some documentary reconstructions as *corrector*; their exact relation/dates are debated.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Some periods of violent revolt caused risk to public position; **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** an alchemical guild, confidential recipe, specialized initiatory station, hazard-based concealment or alchemist censorship was involved in these particular petitions.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
Roman Egyptian regnal formulas are **public dating terminology**, not code for the making of gold. Avoid supplying exact Greek papyrus phrasing without consulting each diplomatic edition. A named **Augustus** is a political, not alchemical, profession.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
The struggle with Diocletian's authority was visible by **297** in dated legal business. Tax/currency reform may have been an economic pressure but its direct causal role is **CONTESTED**; do not state it as documented motive for all rebels.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
The actual recorded response is **local adoption of Domitianus's regnal dates** on documents. Later source accounts of c.298 defeat concern political control; they do not show any documented adaptation of alchemical technique.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
*Original* 297 Egyptian documents: **P.Isid.139/99/62, P.Thead.26, P.Michael.24**; the last's public transcription [DDbDP/Papyri.info via Egyptian Inscriptions](https://egyptianinscriptions.org/object/139753), date 2 Dec 297. **Ancient text date = actual documentary 297**, unlike the later Suda book-burning text. Editions consulted via specialist indexes; not all original facsimiles read.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
A claimant's **recognized administrative use of a title** can be demonstrated in **original documents** even if official sovereignty changes. This supplies a strong comparison to what recognition of an actual alchemical station would require.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Dates of rebellion's opening, duration and succession Achilleus/Domitianus, exact shop effects, original law/edict authorizing suppression of chemical books, any named alchemical official.

## SOURCES: primary, secondary and limits
[PLRE documentary entry](https://connectinglateantiquities.org/text.html?id=plre-1-domitianus-6), [P.Michael.24](https://egyptianinscriptions.org/object/139753), [W25-P01–P02](../08_SOURCE_REGISTER/CE_0281-0300__SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

**Final station test:** Political Augustus recognized locally in original papyri; **NOT** an Alchemist station.
