# ST-083 — 297/298 CE: Domitius Domitianus Strikes Imperial Names into Worked Metal

**Scope:** Roman Egyptian mint comparator, not a named goldmaker. **Date/witness:** original Alexandrian year-two struck coins **297/298**. **Evidence verdict:** ATTESTED object and issuing title, NO SECURE EVIDENCE alchemy. **Window:** [25, 281–300](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0281-0300_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Tetrarchic Egyptian currency administration was contested by Domitius Domitianus during the 297 rebellion, alongside claims made in papyri. Coin legends translate a political assertion into mass-circulated material.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
[RPC X 76414](https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/76414) describes actual **Alexandrian year-two tetradrachms**, mean **6.66 g** for cataloged specimens, obverse **`ΔΟΜΙΤΙΑΝΟϹ ϹΕΒ`**, reverse **`L Β`** and Nike; [RPC X 76417](https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/76417) is another year-two type with a different Nike design. These are evidence for producing/striking alloyed metal **as coinage**, not conversion of copper to gold.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
`Σεβαστός` is an imperial **Augustus** title and Nike depicts **victory** as an idealized political message. Issuing victory imagery is not neutral historical proof of military success, much less the miraculous creation of precious metal.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
The mint presented Domitianus's claim as authoritative on currency despite rivalry with Diocletian. Craftspeople remain **unnamed**. Original papyri demonstrate local regnal-year usage, independently from the coins. Claimant's legal standing and artisan's production expertise must not be conflated.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Control of minting is institutional and economically consequential; the source does **not** establish a secret alchemical guild, recipe monopoly, persecution of metal transmuters, special initiatory code, or expert's motive to conceal. Metalworking skill does not prove a metallic transmutation ideology.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
Greek **`ΔΟΜΙΤΙΑΝΟϹ ϹΕΒ`** = Domitianus styled Augustus. **`L Β`** is *regnal year two*, not special alchemical ratio. **Nike** is public political iconography. Numismatic term **tetradrachm** and alloy labels are catalog classifications; no 297 actor says this issue resulted from `chēmeia`.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
A disputed sovereign and tetrarchic political/monetary competition created motive to issue autonomous currency around **297–298**. The specific intentions of mint workers or how reform triggered rebellion are unverified.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Material coins were actually made bearing the insurgent title and era. The main Roman state later defeated the rebellion, but this case gives **no individualized craftsman's response**, nor a trace of confiscated recipe books.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
Original dated issues preserved as physical specimens in multiple collections, critically cataloged in **RPC X 76414/76417**. An actual coin is not a medieval written eyewitness account of book-burning.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
The monetary station **makes standing publicly visible via material objects** while practical worker identities remain anonymous. Material production, public authorization, and spiritual/transmutational interpretation are separate.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Actual specific alloy composition for each individual specimen (requires assay), employees' names, full fiscal policies, links to ancient recipes, any 297 sanctioned Alchemist.
