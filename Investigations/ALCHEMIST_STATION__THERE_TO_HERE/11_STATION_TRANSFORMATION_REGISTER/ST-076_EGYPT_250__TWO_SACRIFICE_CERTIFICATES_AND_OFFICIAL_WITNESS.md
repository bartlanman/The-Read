# ST-076 — 14/17 June 250: The State Certifies Performed Ritual in Two Independent Egyptian Papyri

**Scope:** Western formation **comparative institutional station**; not an alchemical permit.  
**Original events and sources:** Oxyrhynchus **14 June 250**, **P.Oxy. IV 658 = Yale inv.65**; Theadelphia **17 June 250**, **P.Mich. III 157**.  
**Date of witness:** The original Greek documents themselves are dated 250 CE.  
**Evidence verdict:** **ATTESTED** ritual reports, identities, publicly witnessed procedures, official validation; **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** of an Alchemist station or alchemical enforcement.  
**Linked window:** [Window 23](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0241-0260_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Decius succeeded Philip in 249. Egypt's provincial authorities implemented a requirement to certify sacrificial acts. The actual text of an empire-wide foundational edict has not survived and cannot be reconstructed word for word from administrative certificates.

## CAPACITY: what demonstrably happened
**P.Oxy. IV 658:** Aurelius L[…]thion (damaged name) states he offered sacrifice and libations, tasted offerings, alongside son Aurelius Dioskoros and daughter Aurelia Lais. **P.Mich. III 157:** Aurelius Sakis of Theoxenis, with children Aion and Heras, reports the same classes of acts at Theadelphia. In the latter, Aurelii Serenos and Hermas explicitly attest witnessing sacrifice. **Capacity demonstrated by record:** performance/reporting of a ritual; no physical proof of a chemical operation.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Both petitioners claim conformity; officials validate observation. We cannot determine actual inward belief of applicants, participants' attitudes to coercion or the ritual's theological efficacy from the documents.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Commissioners selected over sacrifices formed a **recognized bureaucratic station** with the ability to certify a specific public performance. Their office, not mystical initiation, lent recognized status to the **record**. No evidence the commissioners could license a metalworker or certify goldmaking.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
**Attested institutional access & coercion:** the ritual petition went to officials appointed for sacrifice and was officially witnessed. **Not demonstrated:** hidden transmutation text, private specialist initiations, craft monopoly, an oath binding artisan secrecy, risk of chemical injury, an Egyptian anti-alchemy campaign. Difference between a participant's compliance and private belief remains unmeasured.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
Theadelphia original: `τοῖς ἐπὶ τῶν θυσιῶν ᾑρημένοις`, those appointed for sacrifices; `ἐθύσαμεν` (we sacrificed), `ἐσπείσαμεν` (we poured libations), `ἐγευσάμεθα` (we tasted); commissioners `εἴδαμεν ὑμᾶς θυσιάζοντας` (we saw you sacrifice). No recipe code or alchemical occupation name.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
Decian imperial religious command reached provincial Egypt by **June 250**, attested through the dates on *original papyrus artifacts*. Do not assume a uniform imperial workflow or conflate “state pressure on ritual” with a dedicated prosecution of alchemists.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Two distinct groups report and receive (or request) official recognition for sacrifice on **June 14 and June 17**. They document actions under new administrative pressure, not all residents' responses. Original statutes, exemption criteria and motivations are not fully accessible here.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
Both are **original-period dated papyri**, not later narratives. [P.Oxy IV 658 transcription](https://egyptianinscriptions.org/object/142907-certificate-of-sacrifice-of-aurelius-l-thion-and-his-children) and [P.Mich III 157 transcription](https://egyptianinscriptions.org/object/139265-certificate-of-sacrifice-of-aurelius-sakis-and-his-children). Yale inv.65 is *same object* as P.Oxy. IV 658, NOT a third independent papyrus.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
**Documented recognition is not equivalent to performance capacity**: an attestation records **who decides that a performed act counts**. This supplies a historical standard for what could authenticate a future Alchemist station, but has no demonstrated alchemical referent.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Exact original Decian edict; broader provincial variations; sincerity of petitioners; social consequences for noncompliance; any alchemical practitioner using these certificates. If an analogy to Alchemist's office is made, label it **comparison**.
