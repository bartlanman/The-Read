# ST-090 — 30 June 331: Orkistos Reclaims Civic Standing and Disputes Cult Payments

**Scope:** municipal authorization comparison, **not Alchemist**. **Original event:** 30 June 331 second imperial response; prior petition **324–326**, first decision **329/330**, separate stages. **Original witness:** composite inscribed stone, CIL III 352+7000 / ILS6091 / MAMA VII305, carved perhaps 331–332. **Verdict:** original public rescript attested; no alchemical connection. [Window 27](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0321-0340_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Orkistos petitioned emperor for status as independent **civitas**, resisting subordination to Nacoleia and money due for certain civic cult obligations. The *original stone dossier* includes different written stages. The first petition/response were NOT both produced in 331.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
Local citizens/council organized petition, presented civic infrastructure, watermills/trades, and sought legal standing; no goldmaking apparatus or a named technical teacher. A watermill's mention does not validate an alchemical industrial station.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Orkistos asserted entitlement to municipal status and relief; emperor recognized some claims. Local prestige and administrative authority emerge from public permission, not transformative-material power.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Constantinian administration adjudicated relations with Nacoleia. The **30 June 331** second rescript addressed obligations and privileges; city status previously conceded. The state validates **municipal office**, not alchemical expertise.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Publicly carved legal records, not secrecy oaths. Economic grievances are visible; no guarded recipes, initiations, danger-based exclusion or persecuted Alchemist network documented.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`civitas` and `pecuniam quam pro cultis ... inferre` concern civic title and money for cults, **not a chemical preparation or priest's occult code**. Embedded earlier documents are not new 331 autograph acts.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
Contested subordination and charges by Nacoleia, argued through petitions over **324–331**, with second specifically dated **331**.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Constantine's **second rescript** confirms public civic station and adjusts liabilities. Not evidence of improved metallurgy or a social standing for an Alchemist.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
**Original physical epigraphic dossier** with documents of multiple dates and a 331 dated reply. ## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
A recognized station can arise when an institution **accepts the petitioner's status and modifies obligations**; independent authorization visible.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
