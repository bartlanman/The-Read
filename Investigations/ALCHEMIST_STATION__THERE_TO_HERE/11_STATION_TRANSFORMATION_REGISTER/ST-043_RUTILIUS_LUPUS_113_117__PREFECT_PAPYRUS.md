# ST-043 — Rutilius Lupus, 113–117 CE: Prefect Recognized in Greek Papyrus

**Original object:** **P.Brem. 4**, dated broadly **113–117 CE**, strategos Apollonios petitions prefect Rutilius Lupus over co-heirs.
**THERE:** Roman provincial legal administration of Egypt.
**CAPACITY:** Written claims and administrative office, not metal transforming.
**PERCEPTION:** Named `Ῥουτιλίωι Λούπωι τῶι κρατίστωι ἡγεμόνι` gives a recognized superior to appeal to.
**AUTHORITY:** The official's title and documents make the station actionable without conferring every craft skill in the province.
**SECRECY/LANGUAGE:** open administrative Greek is not hidden alchemical vocabulary.
**OUTSIDE:** legal property disputes, state administrative needs; **this specific letter not attested as related to 115–117 war**.
**HERE:** Documentary proof of office does not imply technical capacity to operate or supervise chemistry.
**REMAINDER:** exact outcome, whether this prefect was Benevento's 88/89 Isis sponsor.
