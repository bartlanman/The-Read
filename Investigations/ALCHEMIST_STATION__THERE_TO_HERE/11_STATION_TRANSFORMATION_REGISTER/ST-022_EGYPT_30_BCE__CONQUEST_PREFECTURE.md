# ST-022 — Egypt 30 BCE: Royal Station Ends, Prefectural Rule Begins

**Date:** 30 BCE annexation, 31 BCE Actium as antecedent. **Place:** Egypt / Rome.  
**Source:** late imperial *Res Gestae* 27, modern specialist historical/epigraphic analysis.  
**Status:** ATTESTED POLITICAL TRANSFORMATION. Distinct Egyptian alchemical station **NOT ATTESTED**.

## THERE
Ptolemaic monarchs ruled through Egyptian priestly and Greek court traditions, employing administrators and patronizing temples. Cleopatra VII's royal station is politically dependent on alliance with Antony.

## OUTSIDE PRESSURE
Octavian's victory at Actium 31 BCE and conquest 30 BCE; Cleopatra and Antony die.

## RESPONSE
Ptolemaic sovereign kingship ends; Octavian appoints a Roman prefect with responsibility for Egypt. Augustus later claims Egypt was added to Rome's domain in *Res Gestae*.

## LANGUAGE / SECRECY
Augustus's Latin retrospective statement authorizes Roman conquest in public memory; not an occult or alchemical cipher. Repeated imperial imagery in Egyptian temple settings does not prove occupation by trained alchemists.

## HERE
**A sovereign station can cease while subordinate religious, educational, and technical stations may continue under a different appointing authority.** Actual continuity must be demonstrated by each specific institution.

## REMAINDER
Which individual workshop specialists changed employment? Did any technical recipe survive because Romans took custody? No case verified in this window.
