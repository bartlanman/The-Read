## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
CTh XVI.10.22–24 (April/June 423) penalize sacrifice while also protecting peaceful Jews and pagans from extralegal assaults. The state distinguishes a prohibited act from a person who may live peacefully. No named Alchemist, chemical practice or laboratory is mentioned.
