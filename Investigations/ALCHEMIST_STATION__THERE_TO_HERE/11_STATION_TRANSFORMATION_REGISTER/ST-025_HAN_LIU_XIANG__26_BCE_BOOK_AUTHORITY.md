# ST-025 — Liu Xiang, 26 BCE: From Failed Goldmaking to Court Authority over Texts

**Place:** Western Han imperial court.  
**Date:** **26 BCE**, Heping year three (August/autumn), reign of Emperor Cheng.  
**Primary witness:** *Hanshu* Emperor Cheng annals; *Hanshu* 30 Bibliographic Treatise, compiled later (first/second century CE).  
**Prior identity:** Liu Gengsheng (later Liu Xiang), subject of the failed goldmaking case ST-018 in Window 08.  
**Verdict:** actual later recognition as textual scholar, not demonstration of successful transmutation.

## THERE
In Window 08 under Emperor Xuan, Liu had claimed a Huainan manuscript could yield gold, won authority over Shangfang casting, and faced punishment when the method failed. The authentic book and apparatus are lost.

## CAPACITY
In 26 BCE Liu's attested competence is **collating, ordering, comparing, describing and presenting classical, philosophical and poetic texts to the court**. It is not a verified ability to produce gold.

## AUTHORITY
Emperor Cheng orders a coordinated project to recover and collate works. Envoy Chen Nong gathers lost books. Liu, as guanglu dafu, collates classics and literature.

## IMPORTANT DIVISION OF LABOR
- Liu Xiang: 经传, 诸子, 诗赋 — classics/commentary, philosophers, poems/verse.
- Ren Hong: 兵书 — military writings.
- Yin Xian: 数术 — number/technical divination.
- **Li Zhuguo (physician): 方技 — medicine and technical arts texts.**
A court can distinguish **different specialists of knowing about texts** from practitioners of the physical procedure described in those texts.

## SECRECY / LANGUAGE
`校中祕書` indicates working with the imperial internal collection; `方技` is an editorial category, not an encrypted reagent. The project itself involves retrieval and collation, and does not prove the previously hidden Huainan gold book had been located or the recipe worked.

## OUTSIDE PRESSURE
Court concern that books were dispersed, fragmentary or lost, and desire to institutionalize learning and useful knowledge.

## RESPONSE
A previously criticized and even prosecuted technical claimant becomes recognized for literary/textual expertise. A new emperor's mandate provides authority for a *different* capacity.

## HERE
**A failed practical claim need not foreclose future legitimate station grounded in another capability.** Here, custodianship and organization of texts receives support independent of experimental chemical success.

## REMAINDER
What specific lost recipes were or were not retrieved? Did Liu ever revisit Hongbao goldmaking? Does any collated *fangji* text preserve his original arts? Date of his actual bibliographic *Bielu* completion unknown.
