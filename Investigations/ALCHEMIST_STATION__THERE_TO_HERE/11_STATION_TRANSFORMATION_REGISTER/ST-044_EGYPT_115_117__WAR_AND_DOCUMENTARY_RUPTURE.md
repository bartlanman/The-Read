# ST-044 — Egyptian Jewish Diaspora War, 115–117 CE: Real Rupture, No Alchemy Purge Proven

**THERE:** Diverse Jewish communities under Roman authority; unrest and anti-Jewish attacks precede revolt.
**CAPACITY:** Actual everyday family and civic activity in Greek papyri; no attested particular Alchemist station.
**PERCEPTION:** Original private letters reflect fear, need to move relatives and danger in towns; historical writers offer conflicting causal narratives.
**AUTHORITY:** Roman provincial/military rule over communities through conflict and suppression.
**SECRECY/LANGUAGE:** Private emergency correspondence and gaps in surviving archives cannot independently prove chemical encryption or professional suppression.
**OUTSIDE:** Anti-Jewish attacks 112, summer 115; wider uprising and brutal suppression 116–117.
**RESPONSE:** Extensive social, human and institutional losses. Respect actual suffering without converting it into a generic metaphor.
**HERE:** Social catastrophe can disrupt people and records; which specific professional lineages disappeared remains unknown.
**REMAINDER:** Exact regional effects on schools/workshops; **Maria the Jewess not dated to 116**, despite later Zosimos citations.
