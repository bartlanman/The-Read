# ST-051 — 154/155 CE: The Workshop's Legal Existence Without Its Craft

**Original witness:** **P.Tebt. II 613 (TM 20821)**, Greek lease from **Antinoopolis** in Roman Egypt, generally catalogued **155 CE**, or **154/155** from the eighteenth regnal year of Antoninus Pius.
**Source status:** period papyrus/documentary record; particular manufacturing trade **NOT IDENTIFIED**.

## THERE
Antinoopolis was founded by Hadrian in **130 CE**. By 154/155 it has property dealings describing a **house and attached workshops**.

## CAPACITY
A building can accommodate technical production, but the lease does not give a specific **metallurgical, pharmaceutical, dyeing or alchemical operation**, nor any original equipment inventory sufficient to infer one.

## PERCEPTION / AUTHORITY
Landlord, lessee and legal language determine a right to **use and possess premises**, not a certification of trained proficiency.

## SECRECY
There is no documented secret formula, alchemical guild licence, protective restriction against outsiders, or evidence a private lab was being concealed.

## THE OUTSIDE
Legal and economic demand for use of valuable urban premises; inherited Hadrianic city status and Antonine property regime.

## RESPONSE
Original period Greek contract documents workshops as property integrated into ordinary financial dealings.

## HERE
**A technically usable station/place may be historically visible while the actual maker, work and expertise remain unknown.** Access to a productive room is not proof of capacity to execute a specific transformation.

## REMAINDER
Workshop trade, occupants, equipment, outputs, organizational practices and relation to learned authors.
