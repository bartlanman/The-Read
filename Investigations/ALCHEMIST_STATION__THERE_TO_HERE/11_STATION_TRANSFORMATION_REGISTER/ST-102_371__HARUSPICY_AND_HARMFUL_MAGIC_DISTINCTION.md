# ST-102 — May 371: The State Separates Haruspicy from Harmful Magic

**Scope:** Roman ritual/penal station comparator, not proof of licensed Alchemist. **Date:** CTh IX.16.9 **29 May 371**, separate IX.16.10 **6 Dec 371**, IX.16.8 date **365/370/373 CONTESTED**. **Witness:** compiled **438 Theodosian Code**, later copies. **Grade:** DATED LEGAL PRESCRIPTION, not observed individual case. [Window 29](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0361-0380_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Following earlier 357/358 prohibitions on divinatory consultation, Valentinianic policy addressed the differentiation of traditional rites and actually harmful acts amidst fear of imperial intrigue.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The legal text considers `haruspicina` (entrail-divination) distinct from `maleficia` (harmful practices). Whether divination provided reliable predictions is **not** confirmed; neither category implies a material transmutation technique.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
`haruspicinam ego nullum cum maleficiorum causis habere consortium`; `nec haruspicinam reprehendimus, sed nocenter exerceri vetamus`. This expresses state belief in a boundary between acceptable ritual and malicious acts, not public endorsement of alchemy.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
The legal issuer qualifies permission while still restricting harm. The 6 December 371 rule governs accusations involving the senatorial order. Neither establishes an authorized chrysopoeia office.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Regulatory differentiation and potential penalties may generate risk for targeted practitioners, but **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** of a specific Alchemist using coded recipes or running a protected guild in response.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`haruspicina` ≠ `maleficium`. In **IX.16.8**, `mathematicorum tractatus` concerns astrology; uncertain date means don't turn it into one exact 371 policy. Do not misread ritual “elements” as elemental gold physics.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
**371** legal differentiation changes formal ritual legitimacy amid political fear. An Alchemist station's actual encounter remains unverified.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
State expresses limited toleration in prescriptive law; no recorded regional individual ritual or chemical worker responding to it in the inspected sources.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[CTh IX.16.7–10 Latin](https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Constitutiones/CTh09.html); compiled later in **438**, not original contemporary law autograph.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
Roman **legal categories** are not fixed universal labels: ritual practice and malicious magic are differentiated in a named statute. No legal place for Alchemist follows.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Practical enforcement, individual cases, date of IX.16.8, whether named alchemists ever claimed relevant protection.
