# STATION TRANSFORMATION REGISTER

**Purpose:** A longitudinal evidence register linked to the twenty-year THERE → HERE chronology. Identify moments when a *capacity*, its *social perception*, its *authorization*, or the surrounding world changed.

**Do not confuse these different station types:**
- **Institutional office** (king, priest, official)
- **Recognized practical expertise** (metalworker, dyer, physician, ritual expert)
- **Alchemical literary authority** (named commentator, master, claimed ancient author)
- **Alchemist as a historically identifiable profession or category**

An event involving the first three **is not automatically evidence** for the fourth.

## What the register records
**THERE → CAPACITY → PERCEPTION → STATION → EXTERNAL PRESSURE → RESPONSE → TEXT MANIFESTATION → HERE → REMAINDER.**

Each record must include:
- Location, cultural domain and inclusive chronological interval.
- Exact date or undated range; event vs later document date.
- Relevant station and its historically attested *name*.
- What was actually practiced; what others claimed was practiced.
- Who authorized it; who contested or forbade it.
- Secrecy audit and technical/allegorical language audit.
- What external pressure entered and why.
- What changed in response, including *no evidence for a response*.
- Surviving texts / physical witnesses and provenance.
- **Scope guard:** does this explain alchemy, an antecedent institution, or a different parallel tradition?
- Status: **ATTESTED / SOURCE-SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION / CONTESTED / NO SECURE EVIDENCE**.
