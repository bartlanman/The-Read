# ST-059 — Galen's 192 CE Fire: Loss of Tools, Ingredients, and Irreplaceable Texts

**Date:** Spring **192 CE**, Rome Sacred Way and Palatine area.
**Event witness:** Galen's own **`Περὶ ἀλυπίας` / `On Avoiding Distress`**, likely composed **early 193**, first-person ancient report surviving only in later Greek manuscript.
**Status:** WELL-DOCUMENTED FIRST-PERSON REPORT OF MATERIAL LOSS; no proof of deliberate anti-alchemical book burning.

## THERE
A professionally trained physician, imperial court compounder and prolific editor/writer stores medical tools, prepared compounds, expensive drugs, and copies of texts in facilities near the Roman Forum considered safe.

## CAPACITY
Galen retains training and memory, but loses **medical instruments**, scarce **raw/prepared medicinal materials**, **his own notes/copies**, and precious **corrected ancient texts**. Some repairs or replacements are possible; other rare writings are not available from any other source Galen can reach.

## PERCEPTION
He reports extreme financial and practical damage; his literary narrative also highlights calm philosophical resilience. Neither actually preserved copy nor neutral inventory exists for every destroyed volume.

## AUTHORITY / SECRECY
Protected **Temple of Peace / Sacred Way storage** fails in a shared fire. Religious or imperial name on the storage area **does not physically preserve its contents**. Nothing establishes intentional concealment or the burning of an Alchemist guild.

## THE OUTSIDE
Catastrophic urban fire destroys simultaneously deposited assets, in a city whose library and copyist networks made much of Galen's scholarship possible.

## RESPONSE
The practitioner writes about the losses and appears to continue his work, but cannot replace some rare exact scholarly editions and texts. Source does not warrant the assertion that every Galenic work is lost.

## HERE
**The competence of a living expert is not identical to the material conditions needed to reproduce, validate, or teach their work. A reputable repository can become the common failure point.**

## REMAINDER
Original fire causes, names of every lost volume, ingredients and instruments, technical results prevented by loss, actual fate of separate copies.
