# ST-108 — 7 December 396: Prior Privileges of Priests and Hierophants Are Formally Withdrawn

**Scope:** actual **legal classification of ritual professionals**, comparative to Alchemist station. **Issue date:** **7 December 396**, **CTh XVI.10.14**, compiled in **438 Theodosian Code**, copies later. **Grade:** ATTESTED TRANSMITTED NORMATIVE LAW and specifically named office classes; individual implementation uncertain. [Window 30](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0381-0400_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Certain temples and ritual institutions previously held exemptions/privileges for priests, assistants, hierophants and other ritual officeholders under older laws.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
Roles imply ritual leadership/service and official eligibility. The law does **not** independently demonstrate effective divination, elemental gold synthesis or membership in a workshop.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Law treats earlier cult-officer privileges as unjustified under new religious policy, indicating the offices **were publicly intelligible enough to name and disallow**.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
`Privilegia si qua concessa sunt antiquo iure sacerdotibus ministris praefectis hierofantis sacrorum ... penitus aboleantur`: **state retracts earlier legal privileges**. A real station-level change, even if no list of named people is supplied.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Loss of legal advantage might pressure such officeholders, **but no source connects a specific goldmaking expert to this class or shows they encrypted recipes in response**.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`sacerdotes` priests, `ministri` attendants, `hierofantae` sacred-revelation experts, `privilegia` prior exemptions. No occupational “Alchemist,” no identical ancient title to author of Synesius dialogue.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
By **396** imperial government explicitly invalidates favorable legal standing of named ritual offices; precise personal effects remain case-specific.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Legal withdrawal ordered, actual staff/payroll outcomes unverified. Alchemical specialists' response **NO SECURE EVIDENCE**.
