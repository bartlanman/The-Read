# ST-086 — Aphrodisias 301 CE: Goldworking Has an Imperial Economic Station, Not an Alchemist's Credential

**Scope:** comparative Roman craft/economic station, not a proven Alchemist event.  
**Original event/witness:** **301 CE**, Latin Edict on Maximum Prices on **Civil Basilica at Aphrodisias**, partial original stone; modern revised composite edition Crawford et al. **2023**.  
**Evidence grade:** **ATTESTED original inscription** for imperial market regulation; **SOURCE-SUPPORTED** reconstructed occupational details from combined copies; **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** of a named state Alchemist.  
**Linked Window:** [26, 301–320 CE](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0301-0320_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Tetrarchic imperial authorities addressed prices, wages and coin values. A physical copy of **Diocletian's AD 301 Edict of Maximum Prices** was carved on the Civil Basilica in **Aphrodisias, Caria**. Surviving fragments represent approximately 40% of the edict's complete reconstructed text, with other fragmentary copies from eastern Roman cities. This original object documents regulatory publicness rather than a legendary clandestine atelier.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The composite **price/wage schedule** recognizes **gold, gold-working and gold-leaf/metal thread-related crafts** alongside other goods and services. These are techniques applied to existing matter and specialist labor transactions; a tariff does **not** prove all operations were performed by the named city, any worker synthesized elemental gold, or a particular goldmaking formula. A goldworker's ability to beat/shape gold is materially different from producing it from copper.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
The imperial decree treats precious metals and specialist craft output as identifiable objects of market value and worker compensation, thus public categories intelligible to authorities and consumers. The law establishes ceiling prices, not a survey of what all artisans could accomplish; no Alchemist is named.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Imperial pricing power recognizes **an economic station** and its transactions. Labor/commodity regulation is not an examination, apprenticeship, license, guild charter or independently validated transmutation claim. Local enforcement details in individual workshops not directly witnessed by stone.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
**ATTESTED PUBLIC economic classification.** No independently dated guild oath, proprietary alchemical cipher, chemical danger-based ban, personal persecution or public Alchemist certificate. Higher skill requirements are plausible but the tariff is neither a secret manual nor proof of particular taught mastery.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`aurum` means gold in a Latin economic schedule; amounts/wages are ceilings, **not magical ingredient ratios**. The **Aphrodisias copy is fragmentary**; the 2023 edition supplies readings and integrations from other witnesses. Never claim a damaged line on this particular stone contains pristine complete evidence for a specialty without specifying edition/reconstruction. No contemporary Latin or Greek official category “Alchemist” is established in this record.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
Economic pressures and imperial monetary reform contextualize **301** pricing intervention. A distinct **Currency Dossier** inscribed at the same basilica is related to coin/value regulation but is **not** the price edict and not the alleged later story of book-burning.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
State publishes maximum prices for tradable crafts. Public inscription is **real administrative action**. Whether a named artisan complied, hid techniques, abandoned a workshop or resisted is **NO SECURE EVIDENCE**. Later assessments of failure/limited enforcement cannot be converted into a local goldworker's documented response.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
**AD 301 original stone inscription**, Civil Basilica north façade, [Oxford Aphrodisias inscriptions](https://aphrodisias.classics.ox.ac.uk/inscriptions.html). [Michael Crawford et al., *Diocletian's Edict of Maximum Prices at the Civil Basilica in Aphrodisias* (2023), DOI 10.29091/9783752002362](https://doi.org/10.29091/9783752002362); [Eleanor Dickey's source-critical review (2024)](https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/2024/2024.07.21/).

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
**Actual public regulation of material-specialist work** can be established even without identifying the individual craftspeople. This is the *economic profession* comparator for whatever later evidence could establish an Alchemist's institutional station. It is not the third-century Diocletian alchemical-book-purge story.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Individual workers and their methods; number of active workshop types in Aphrodisias; exact local gold tariff details on surviving stone vs composite; actual efficacy/enforcement; any contemporary person authorized to *transmute* base material into gold.
