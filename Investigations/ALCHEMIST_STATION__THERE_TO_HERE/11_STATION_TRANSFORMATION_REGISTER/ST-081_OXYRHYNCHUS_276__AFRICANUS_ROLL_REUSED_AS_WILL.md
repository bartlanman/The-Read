# ST-081 — 276 CE: An Earlier Julius Africanus Roll Becomes a Legal Will

**Scope:** independent material-textual **comparison case** and **cross-window continuation of ST-071**, not a duplicated independent recipe manuscript or 276 Alchemist.  
**Event date:** Oxyrhynchus **276 CE**, specifically **25 June–24 July 276** per Kyprianos for the Greek manuscript reuse.  
**Manuscript witness:** **the SAME original object**, British Library **Papyrus 2040**; `P.Oxy. III 412` / `P.Lond.Lit 174` on **recto**, `P.Oxy. VI 907` on **verso**.  
**Evidence verdict:** **ATTESTED** original roll reused, published 276 legal copy, earlier book fragment; **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** identifiable 276 Alchemist, book owner or clandestine knowledge network.  
**Linked window:** [Window 24](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0261-0280_CE__WINDOW.md), earlier [ST-071](ST-071_KETOI_BOOK_18_OXYRHYNCHUS__PAPYRUS_AND_276_WILL.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
**Julius Africanus**, an earlier named early-third-century author of the mixed technical/literary *Kestoi* (dedicated Severus Alexander 222–235), is known via later fragments and actual original-period papyrus. His **Book 18** physically exists in two columns on the front of BL Papyrus 2040, palaeographically dated **broadly 225–275/276**, **not exact 276**. A Roman Egyptian city's legal inheritance regime provides new demand for documentary surface.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
Someone **copied**/wrote a formal 276 testamentary document on the back of an older bookroll. Papyrus **recto** writing runs with fibers, **verso** across fibers and upside down. It was a physical act of **reuse and legal record-making**, not transmutation of the papyrus into a different substance, a reconstitution of an alchemical laboratory technique, or proof of personal goldmaking.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
The legal copy expresses rights and appointments for property/inheritance of **Hermogenes alias Eudaemon**, identified as **councillor/officeholder**. The surviving object does not establish whether testator, family, scribe or previous roll-owner interpreted the *Kestoi*, approved of necromancy, practiced transmutation or wished to conceal anything.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Legal office and social property position for Hermogenes are explicitly named in British Library catalog; five children (three sons, two daughters), wife, inherited real property and enslaved persons, guardianship and dowries appear. This is an **identifiable property/legal station** distinct from Africanus's earlier authorial status. This reused papyrus is not an imperial permission for alchemy.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Original copy's 225–275/276 date range cannot establish deliberate secrecy in 276. Physical reuse **might** arise from access to material, practical convenience or discard, but motives are **NO SECURE EVIDENCE**. The lost geometrical-musical ingredient key discussed in other *Kestoi* fragments is **not present** on the physically surviving Book 18 portion: absolutely do not conflate *loss of explanatory appendix* with *reused roll* or *anti-alchemical suppression*.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
**`Kestoi`** is earlier author's literary title; **`P.Oxy. III 412`** and **`P.Oxy. VI 907`** are distinct **modern editions of opposite sides of one ancient object**. The verso is a **legal Greek will**, not a re-keyed alchemical cipher. The manuscript's physical inversion is direct evidence of **different writing acts**.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
**276 documentary/inheritance needs** reshaped this roll's actual material function; a previously inscribed surface acquired another text in a different genre. There is no demonstrated external order to erase Africanus or burn alchemical books. The broader cultural process of **reusing older writing supports** is attested by this object's direct material witness but local economic motives remain unverified.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
An unidentified writer reused the existing papyrus for a 276 legal document. The earlier literary text **survives on the other side**, showing reuse can **preserve material traces** irrespective of original author's prestige. No response to persecution, secret technique or proof of deliberate memory protection established.

**Earlier composition:** `Kestoi` Book 18 by **Sextus Julius Africanus**, earlier c.222–235 corpus. **Original surviving copy date:** broadly **225–275/276**. **Later writing:** `P.Oxy. VI 907` = will of Hermogenes alias Eudaemon, **276**. These links describe **one object**, two text sides, not independent confirmation of a professional guild.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
**A text's survival may be a by-product of its carrier's new non-literary use**. Authorial fame, symbolic recipe style, actual copied Book 18, will of different officeholder, and modern museum recovery occupy separate temporal and social stations.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Exact prior copy date, who possessed original bookroll, ownership connection to Hermogenes, why unused surface was available, whether other rolls of the *Kestoi* were present in Egypt 276, how often such reuse occurs, lost appendix contents, any named alchemist.
