# ST-052 — 157 CE: Galen's Recognized Medical Station

**Period:** Galen's training primarily c.143–157; appointment **157 CE** as **physician to gladiators in Pergamum**.
**Evidence class:** ancient biographical/autobiographical writings authored largely by **Galen himself** and transmitted in later manuscripts; specialist chronology in *The Oxford Handbook of Galen* (2024). **Not an original surviving employment letter from 157**.
**Verdict:** a comparatively well-reconstructed named medical practitioner, **not a documented Alchemist**.

## THERE
Hellenic-Roman medical and philosophical education involved teachers, differing schools and competing reputations for practical care.

## CAPACITY
Galen reports studies in philosophy and anatomy and training under named teachers, including Satyrus and Pelops. After travel in Smyrna, Corinth and Alexandria, he receives gladiatorial medical work at Pergamum. This is **healing and anatomy**, not successful base-metal-to-gold transformation.

## PERCEPTION / AUTHORITY
A named training route, medical appointment, patient population and substantial authored corpus make professional identity much easier to reconstruct than a pseudonymous alchemical author. His own assessments of clinical results, however, are interested testimony and **not independent modern validation**.

## SECRECY / LANGUAGE
Technical medical vocabulary, named teachers and clinical practice show specialized learned capacity. That does not imply initiation into a secret goldmaking school.

## THE OUTSIDE
Competition for medical credibility, need to treat wounds and manage gladiator injuries; social value in diagnostic and practical proficiency.

## RESPONSE
Galen's office in 157 provides professional recognition. His more famous **Rome demonstrations from 162 CE** belong in **the next window**, not this one.

## HERE
**A recognizable professional station can be supported by training, an accountable role, work on identifiable patients and later written claims.** Each evidence strand has its own bias and degree of independence.

## REMAINDER
Independent contract of employment, exact training dates, full record of actual outcomes, which claims are autobiographical rather than external corroboration.
