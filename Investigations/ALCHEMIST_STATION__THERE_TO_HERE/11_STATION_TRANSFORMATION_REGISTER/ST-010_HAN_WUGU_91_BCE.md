# ST-010 — 91 BCE Witchcraft Crisis: Political Danger Is Not Proof of an Anti-Alchemist Purge

**Geography:** Western Han imperial court  
**Date:** 91 BCE, Window 06 (100–81 BCE)  
**Witness:** *Hanshu* 45, composed in first/second century CE, not an immediate 91 BCE eyewitness transcript  
**Status:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED account of official accusations, political destruction and coerced confession. Alchemist target **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

## THERE
Earlier Han court actively patronized *fangshi* and even executed some when their promises failed; imperial family and court competition remained intense.

## CAPACITY AND PERCEPTION
Jiang Chong's accusations concern *wugu* 巫蠱 curse practices, objects, figures, and threatening supernatural effects. The accusation could be politically effective without an actual harmful ritual having worked. The source recounts false accusation and coercion.

## AUTHORITY
The emperor appoints Jiang Chong as an investigator. The figure acting from an authorized official station acquires the power to define which practices count as dangerous, and accusations destabilize the crown prince's position.

## SECRECY / DANGER
The source documents *actual political danger* from suspicion of forbidden rites and false proof. It does **not** show a carefully concealed metal-transmutation recipe or a blanket persecution of chemically competent practitioners.

## LANGUAGE
*Wugu* is a culturally specific category of harmful curse/magic, not an interchangeable synonym for *fangshi* and not a historical name for an Alchemist.

## THE OUTSIDE
Dynastic rivalry, fear of succession, sick emperor, investigations and accusations.

## RESPONSE
Torture, fear, revolt, executions and the collapse of official trust in some court actors; later recognition that Jiang Chong's conduct was fraudulent. These are effects on political station.

## HERE
**Control over the accusation of dangerous knowledge can give an official power to exclude and destroy other people, irrespective of whether the accused possessed that knowledge.**

## REMAINDER
No documented alchemical line targeted; causal relationship to a supposed broad suppression of alchemy unknown.
