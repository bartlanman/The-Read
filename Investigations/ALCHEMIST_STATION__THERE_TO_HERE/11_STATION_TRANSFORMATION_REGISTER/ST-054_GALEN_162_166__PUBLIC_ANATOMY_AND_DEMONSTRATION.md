# ST-054 — Galen in Rome, 162–166 CE: A Practitioner Makes a Causal Claim Visible

**Place/date:** Rome, 162–166 CE (a specific 163 date is a modern scholarly reconstruction).
**Primary witness:** Galen's own descriptions of anatomical demonstrations in surviving later manuscripts. Critically contextualized by modern scholarship on public medical rivalry.
**Grade:** known historical physician, strong **self-authored** testimony to professional acts. No independently recovered contemporary 163 demonstration log.

## THERE
Galen had taken gladiatorial medical office in Pergamum (157 CE). In Rome a medical elite valued explanation, diagnosis, philosophy, practical performance and public argument.

## CAPACITY
Galen performed animal anatomical demonstrations and reports interventions related to the **recurrent laryngeal nerve**, in which altering the nerve affects vocalization. These are physiological demonstrations, not chemical gold-making procedures. A public animal experiment could show an effect while leaving the entire causal interpretation, methodological biases and transfer to human anatomy debatable.

## PERCEPTION
An audience saw what the demonstrator showed them; Galen narrated the causal argument and used the event to build his professional reputation. The rhetoric of mastery and the underlying physiological effect should be judged separately.

## AUTHORITY AND SECRECY
A known physician with named education can contest the reputation of other physicians through repeatable-looking public display rather than mere master-name inheritance. The intervention was presented **publicly**, not encoded as a secret initiation manual. The exact procedures and audience response survive chiefly through Galen's own accounts.

## THE OUTSIDE / RESPONSE
Competition among Roman experts presses a practitioner to make knowledge convincing in front of other people; rhetorical staging and selective reporting remain possible.

## HERE
**Demonstrable capacity, the authority to interpret the result, and the audience's recognition are separate even when all three occur in one public event.** Recognized expertise is neither limitless nor equivalent to a new Alchemist office.

## REMAINDER
Independent audience testimony, precisely dated demonstrations, modern replication under ethical non-animal methods, which inferences depended on species differences.
