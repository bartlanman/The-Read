# ST-078 — Dionysius's Egyptian Magi: A Hostile Label Is Not an Alchemist's Certificate

**Scope:** Perception and classification control case, **NOT a proven Greek alchemical station**.  
**Event claim:** Valerian's policy turn, c.257–258.  
**Composition/witness:** Dionysius, *letter to Hermammon*, composed retrospectively in mid-late third century; excerpts quoted by Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* VII.10.3–6, **early fourth century**; later textual manuscripts.  
**Evidence verdict:** **ATTESTED** as a polemical textual allegation; **CONTESTED/NO SECURE EVIDENCE** for the allegation's literal institutional and material meaning.  
**Linked window:** [Window 23](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0241-0260_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
During the Valerian persecutions, Christian leaders constructed explanations for why an emperor previously perceived as relatively accommodating became repressive. Such accounts frame rival learned, ritual and political actors through hostile language.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
A person in the transmitted passage, associated with **Macrianus**, is described as a teacher/ruler of a circle of Egyptian `magoi` and an influence on Valerian. **No original alchemical workshop, apparatus, operation, demonstrated recipe or made-gold object is linked to the charge.**

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Dionysius attributes changes in policy to sinister influence and describes abhorrent ritual crimes. This documents **a Christian author's perception/accusation**, not the historical truth of every act or causal chain. Eusebius quotes it generations later.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
The allegation is about access to imperial power. Outside independent documents are needed to establish the precise alleged religious office and its relation to imperial decisions. Political counsel or advancement alone does not turn an actor into a material-transformation technician.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
**No reliable evidence** from this text for trade-guild secrecy, an alchemical master oath, hiding chemical recipes to avoid persecution, or trained capacity in elemental transmutation. The narrative's imagery of concealed incantations is **hostile representation**, not empirical investigation of laboratories.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
The English rendering **“Magi from Egypt”** translates a rhetorically loaded ancient label; it is not contemporaneous self-identification as **`chēmeutai`**, and Greek `magos` may designate many stereotypes of ritual/scholarly illegitimacy. Dufault (2019, **pp.19–38, 51–69**) analyzes the “learned sorcerer” as a socioliterary category. The vocabulary may establish polemic, not occupational title.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
Valerian persecution and an imperial power struggle shape which forms of authority seem legitimate. The **accusation itself** is a reaction to Outside pressure experienced by Christians, not evidence that the alleged magoi adapted metallurgy in response.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Dionysius rhetorically discredits the rumored adversary and vindicates the persecuted community. **No direct identifiable response by a craftworker or a Greek alchemical teacher**.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[Dionysius via Eusebius, *HE* VII.10.3–6](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250107.htm), written in his later ecclesiastical historian's selection; **not** an original 257 imperial accusation or excavated script. [Olivier Dufault 2019](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2ks0g83x), for `magos` stigma and scholarly patronage.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
**An adversary's reputation may be historically visible earlier than the adversary's actual techniques.** An office cannot be recovered by turning the enemy's label into a secure title. This negative result preserves the difference between **perceived authority** and **material capacity**.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Whether the named political figure performed any ritual; actual organized circle; specific imperial causality; exact wording before Eusebius; any relation to alchemical recipes, Maria or Zosimos; further independent documentary corroboration.

## SOURCES
[Eusebius HE VII.10](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250107.htm), quoted Dionysius; [Dufault *Early Greek Alchemy*](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2ks0g83x); [Window 23 register T05](../08_SOURCE_REGISTER/CE_0241-0260__SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

**Final station test:** **A retrospective polemical label contesting ritual/political reputation, not positive proof of the existence or transformation of an Alchemist station.**
