# ST-013 — c.88 BCE Egyptian Dynastic Turnover: Political Station Without Alchemical Lineage

**Region:** Ptolemaic Egypt  
**Date:** Ptolemy IX returns to Alexandria c.88 BCE; exact phase uncertain. Death of Ptolemy IX possibly in Dec 81 BCE or early 80 BCE, **boundary spanning two windows**.  
**Primary witnesses:** papyri, coin series and inscriptions in scholarly chronology; no alchemical manual assigned.  
**Verdict:** POLITICAL STATION CHANGE attested, ALCHEMICAL CONNECTION UNKNOWN.

## THERE
Ptolemy X ruled in Egypt under continuing temple, royal and fiscal structures, after Cleopatra III died. Ptolemy IX remained rival claimant.

## OUTSIDE PRESSURE / RESPONSE
Political resistance and dynastic competition lead to expulsion of Ptolemy X and restoration of Ptolemy IX c.88 BCE. Different sources place stages of that return differently.

## CAPACITY / PERCEPTION / LANGUAGE
Public royal legitimacy marked in coins, Demotic/Greek administrative documents and temple titulature. None is a technical proof of alchemical transmutation or a sealed chemical lexicon.

## SECRECY
Political claims and hidden motives cannot be generalized into a secret Egyptian goldmakers guild.

## HERE
The occupant and official name of sovereign station can change while no record demonstrates a corresponding specialist craft station change.

## REMAINDER
The precise death of Ptolemy IX cannot responsibly be placed definitely in this window; source permits late 81 or early 80 BCE. Next window starts at 80 BCE and should carry boundary cross-reference. No verified direct Greek/Chinese transmission.
