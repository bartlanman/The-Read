# ST-093 — 2 August 337: Artisan Privileges Issued in Constantine's Name AFTER His Death?

**Scope:** trade capacity/transmission, contested imperial legal attribution, NOT an Alchemist license. **Issue date preserved:** **2 August 337**. **Emperor death:** **22 May 337**. **Sources:** *CTh* 13.4.2, *CJ* 10.66.1, later code copies. **Grades:** attested transmitted legal text and roster; **CONTESTED ORIGINAL ISSUER**, implementation; no named Alchemist. [Window 27](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0321-0340_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Late Constantinian imperial administration recognized economic importance of specialized artisans. Constantine's death preceded formal succession changes, making legal administrative signatures complex in summer 337.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
Law directs exemption for specified artisans from civic obligations `munera` **so they can become more skilled and teach sons**: `peritiores fieri et suos filios erudire`. *CJ* lists **`aurifices`, `deauratores`, `argentarii`, `aerarii`, `fusores`, `vitrearii`**, among more crafts. This is an explicit **legal intention to sustain and transmit competence**, not records of successful apprenticeship or elemental transformation.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Imperial legal framers saw technical skill as valuable and vulnerable to compulsory obligations. Protected time for learning was considered worthwhile. Whether artisan households benefited cannot be established from quoted law.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
*Theodosian* heading `idem Augustus` relates to prior Constantine law; *Justinianic* records Constantine name. Yet he died **22 May 337**, while subscription says **2 August**. Historian R.W. Burgess (2008) argues **posthumous issuance in Constantine's name**; another possibility is later codification/heading or dating error. **Do not report Constantine personally issued while alive in August**.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Exemption is publicly transmitted; specific listed trades were socially valuable. No guarded chrysopoeia formula, named initiatory order, Alchemist title, chemical safety secrecy restriction or persecution response documented. Sons' teaching is a law's **goal**, not a proven father-to-son transmission event.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`Artifices artium ... ab universis muneribus vacare praecipimus ... peritiores fieri et suos filios erudire`. **`Munera`** civic duties not universally all taxes. **`Aurifices`** goldsmiths, not alchemists; `deauratores` gilders. An authorizing name can outlive ruler's life; **no direct link to literary pseudonymity** in Greek alchemy, only methodological comparison.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
Need to retain skill in cities vs obligation burden by **2 August 337**; emperor's death and succession context create attribution uncertainty.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Normative governmental response: enumerated artisan **munera relief** intended for schooling. Individual uptake/outcome unknown. No demonstrated response of a Greek alchemical workshop.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[*CTh* 13.4.2](https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/theodosius/theod13.shtml) later fifth-century compilation; [*CJ* 10.66.1 text and appended list](https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Corpus/CJ10.htm) sixth-century compilation; neither contemporary original 337 tablet. [R.W. Burgess 2008 *The Summer of Blood*](https://constantinethegreatcoins.com/articles/Burgess_THE_SUMMER_OF_BLOOD_The_Great_Massacre_of_337_and_the_Promotion_of_the_Sons_of_Constantine.pdf).

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
Technical capacity can receive **public protection for its continued transmission**, beyond simple right to sell craft goods; imperial legal authorizing attribution requires independent date controls.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
True original issuer/date, posthumous policy decision processes, implementation and privilege beneficiaries, scope of exemptions, actual family skills, Alchemist training/chemical success.
