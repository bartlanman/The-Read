# ST-045 — 88/89 to 117 CE: Rutilius Lupus Same-Person Identity WATCH

**STATUS: CONTESTED, NOT CLOSED.**
**THERE:** **88/89 CE** Benevento obelisks name Isis patron **Rutilius Lupus**.
**POSSIBLE CROSSING:** **M. Rutilius Lupus** (Roman grain prefect, brickworks proprietor associated with **110/116 CE** bricks, Egyptian prefect **113–117 CE**). Scholars have proposed that the donor and prefect were the same; similar Rutilian family names mean the identity does NOT follow from matching name alone.
**CAPACITY:** Sacred patronage, industrial ownership, administrative power and practical artisanship are separable.
**PERCEPTION:** A compelling narrative of moving 'home→factory→Egypt' can seduce investigators into inventing a lineage.
**AUTHORITY:** Specific inscriptions/papyri authenticate each office; only independent biographical evidence can authenticate **same person**.
**LANGUAGE/SECRECY:** Names encoded in Egyptian hieroglyphs versus Latin industry stamps versus Greek petitions; different access and interpretations.
**OUTSIDE:** Roman empire's interlocking patronage, supply and public offices.
**HERE:** Preserve identity as a research hypothesis; **even identity confirmation would not prove Alchemist status**.
**REMAINDER:** `PIR² R252`, praenomen, filiation, dates, estates, independent family records.
