# ST-120 — 438/439 code promulgation

**Status:** period-relevant evidentiary comparator, NOT positive proof of a named Alchemist. **Linked Window:** [421–440 CE](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0421-0440_CE__WINDOW.md). **Source control:** [Window 32 source register](../08_SOURCE_REGISTER/CE_0421-0440__SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Theodosius II's legal code promulgated February 438; Roman Senate proceedings on 25 December 438 record reception; general effective date January 1, 439. Senate proceedings survive later copied text; some code books survive in early medieval manuscripts. One must distinguish original law, excerpt, enactment of corpus, and physical surviving witness.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
recognized legal code and text curator. Documentary legislation prescribes or recognizes acts; independent practical competence cannot be inferred for every person in a category.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
State-sanctioned arrangement and republication creates binding authority and redefines what inherited text can legally say. The category actually authorized or challenged is **recognized legal code and text curator**, not the imagined ability to turn one chemical element into another.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Neither the source nor its copy chain proves an alchemical oath, deliberate metal-recipe cipher, danger-restricted initiation or hidden guild. A legal prohibition or a value standard must be assessed by its expressly named object.
