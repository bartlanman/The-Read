# ST-098 — 357–358: The Forbidden Astrologer Is Not the Protected Geometer

**Scope:** comparative divinatory/political station, not alchemical suppression. **Date:** CTh IX.16.4 **25 Jan 357**, IX.16.5 **4 Dec 357 [?]**, IX.16.6 **5 Jul 358**. **Witness:** laws in the *Theodosian Code* (438) and later copies. **Grade:** ATTESTED prohibition texts, **NO SECURE EVIDENCE convicted Alchemist**. [Window 28](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0341-0360_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Imperial court politics and predictions about future rulers raised official anxiety about diviners; the state publicly valued practical surveyors/geometers by **CTh XIII.4.3 (344)**.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The forbidden acts include consulting entrail-diviners (`haruspices`), **astrologers (`mathematici`)**, soothsayers, magi, malefici and dream interpreters; this documents **how imperial authorities classified alleged practitioners**, NOT validated ability to predict or disturb nature. No evidence of a named metal-recipe author.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
The law treats `divinandi curiositas` as dangerous. IX.16.5 alleges magical operators `elementa turbare` and do harm. Such accusations are **claims**, not measured metallic conversions or witnessed chemical capacities.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Constantius's issued/legal authority seeks to prohibit practices by penalties including execution/torture, with IX.16.6 addressing **the imperial court**. No 357 dated court ledger names a Greek alchemical writer convicted.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
These laws could create fear among targeted divination practitioners, but **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** an alchemical guild adapted its language to evade them. No trade-secret, apprentice oath or toxic reagent protection established.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`mathematicus` in **IX.16.4** is **astrologer**, not engineering **`geometra` in XIII.4.3**. `magus` is a charged administrative/polemical word, not equivalent to a chemical transmuter. `elementa turbare` accuses disturbance of nature; it is not proof chemical elements were transformed.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
Legal penalties for consultation and allegedly harmful magical practice dated **357–358** shift what categories of predictive/ritual work the state permits.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
The government's **response** is normative condemnation and defined punishments. A particular targeted actor's response or actual execution needs independent attestation. No alchemical technique/mentor is observed changing behavior.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
