# ST-093 — 2 August 337: An Artisan Exemption Is Dated After Its Named Emperor's Death

**Scope:** legal craft specialization and intergenerational training, comparative to (NOT positive evidence for) an Alchemist. **Event/document subscription:** **2 August 337**, transmitted **CTh 13.4.2 / CJ 10.66.1**. **Emperor's death:** Constantine **22 May 337**. **Witness:** fifth-/sixth-century compilations and later physical manuscripts; **NO original 337 stone**. **Verdict:** **ATTESTED LAW TEXT; CONTESTED SENDER**, implementation/Alchemist link **NO SECURE EVIDENCE**. [Window 27](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0321-0340_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
The Roman state assessed civic `munera` and specialized occupations in a period of transition after Constantine's death. Craftworkers represented useful technical labor with skills that required time and teaching to sustain.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The preserved list in **CJ 10.66.1** identifies **`aurifices` goldsmiths**, `deauratores` gilders, `argentarii` silversmiths, `fusores` founders, `aerarii` bronze-workers, `vitrearii` glassworkers, `fullones` fullers, architects, physicians, makers and other artisans. These are capable-practice occupational categories but the text does not document any **named worker** or successful new elementary substance being produced.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
The legal instruction explicitly says release from burdens would allow workers **`peritiores fieri`** (become more skilled) and **`filios erudire`** (educate sons). Government values **increased capacity and generational transmission**, but we lack proof that every artisan improved or taught a son.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
`Artifices artium brevi subdito comprehensarum ... ab universis muneribus vacare praecipimus`: formal exemptions from civic obligations prescribed for listed crafts. Not blanket tax-free status, professional examination or an `Alchemist` charter. **`Constantinus` is the heading in later codes**, while law subscription is after his death. **Burgess (2008)** argues plausible posthumous issuance in his name; **Lenski (2016)** questions assuming codex `idem` reliably identifies signer. **Do not assert deceased emperor personally enacted it**; provenance alternatives preserved.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
No protected proprietary recipe, government cryptographic alchemy key, hazard restriction, or persecution of specialists is recorded. Public exemptions are designed to encourage teaching, not prove that every craft's knowledge was secret or hereditary in practice.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`munera` = compulsory civic obligations; `artifices` craft specialists; `aurifices` working existing gold, not necessarily manufacturing elemental gold; `deauratores` gilders; `filios erudire` intended sons' training, not empirical proof of a teaching lineage. Date `IIII Non. Aug. Feliciano et Titiano conss.` = **2 Aug 337**; authorship heading conflict **CONTESTED**.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
Civic service burdens could interfere with needed training and transmission. The **337 prescribed exemption** is the attested policy response. Succession after **22 May 337** creates a separate **authority/attribution problem**; neither proves effect on an alchemical guild.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Law calls for exemption. Specific local implementation, named goldsmiths, money value of exemptions, apprenticeship and outcomes **UNVERIFIED**. No individual alchemical writer can be connected to the promulgation.

Codifications **438/6th c**, later copies, not original 337 signed artifact.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
The legal station recognizes not simply **an existing occupation** but institutional conditions for its **learning and transfer**. A named ruler's attributed legal authority can continue in **later text** despite chronology making his personal direct action impossible at date as subscribed.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Exact issuing sovereign, date of drafting vs posting, editorial history of imperial header, actual exempt gold-workers and sons, effects on work, any alchemist self-description or actual transmutation.
