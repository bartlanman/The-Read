# ST-095 — 6 July 344: The Protected Geometer and Technical Teacher

**Scope:** Roman educational/technical station comparator, NOT a named Alchemist. **Issue date:** 6 July 344. **Witness:** *Codex Theodosianus* XIII.4.3 compiled 438, later manuscript copies; **not an excavated 344 original.** **Grade:** ATTESTED transmitted legislation; no demonstrable actual beneficiary. [Window 28](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0341-0360_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Mid-fourth-century Roman state sought water engineering, measuring and construction specialists, following earlier 334/337 artisan-training policies. Constantius II and Constans are named imperial senders to prefect Leontius.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The law enumerates mechanical division/cutting, geometry, architecture and **aqueduct grading/leveling** (`aquarum ... ductus`). These are claimed teachable operations, **not physical proof that each engineer carried them out or that gold was created from copper**.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
Governors classified reliable measurement as societally useful, distinguishable from illicit predictive astrology in later laws. The claim that enough instructors exist to teach comes from the **law**, not independent 344 education rosters.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
`mechanicos et geometras et architectos` are protected through `inmunitates` and encouraged into `docendi adque discendi`. Such legal authorization **does not** contain `chēmeia` or a title equivalent to Alchemist.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Learning/teaching is explicitly encouraged in the public legal prescription, not proof of closed secret initiation. **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** of recipes withheld for commercial or safety purposes or fear of prosecution.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`geometra` = practical geometer in engineering context; `mechanicus` = mechanical practitioner; neither equals `mathematicus` when that word means astrologer in **CTh IX.16.4 (357)**. `inmunitates` are prescribed exemptions, not guaranteed career success.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
Presumed need for infrastructure knowledge and specialist instruction drove the **344 legal protection**. No economic statistics for the program's efficacy.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
State creates a protected legal environment for teaching skilled measurement and engineering. **Actual named teachers, students, and completed aqueducts as a direct consequence unverified**.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[CTh XIII.4.3 original Latin in later codification](https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Constitutiones/CTh13.html); issue subscription **344 July 6**, compilation **438**, later copies. This is **not an original 344 engraving**.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
A publicly protected station defined by technical **operation and teachability** exists in the law, providing a comparative standard to test alchemical knowledge claims against—not an official chemical school.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Independent beneficiaries, actual enforcement, syllabus, personal skill, alchemical links, manuscript copying and apprenticeship.

## SOURCES: primary, secondary, limits
[W28-T01](../08_SOURCE_REGISTER/CE_0341-0360__SOURCE_REGISTER.md). No period witness assigning an Alchemist title.

**Final test:** maintenance/expansion of technical-learning station, NO Alchemist.
