# ST-101 — Julian 362: Municipal/Imperial Recognition of Teachers, Not Alchemical Licensure

**Scope:** comparative educated teacher station, not an Alchemist. **Event:** legal constitution **17 June 362** with separate Greek explanatory letter after that date. **Surviving witnesses:** CTh XIII.3.5 in **438 Theodosian Code** and later manuscripts; Julian Greek *Letter 36* (Wright) / *61c* (Bidez), different chain. **Grade:** DATED TRANSMITTED LAW / DISTINCT AUTHOR LETTER, no identified alchemical teacher. [Window 29](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0361-0380_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Julian attempted to organize teaching in the cities around moral/religious seriousness in classical education. Teachers carried cultural authority in the interpretation of canonical Greek poets and public values.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
The Latin statute recognizes `magistri studiorum doctoresque` and demands municipal council judgment and imperial review. This is a **procedure for approval**, not an examination demonstrating a named teacher's ability or an evaluation of any metal recipe.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
In a **different Greek explanatory text**, Julian says teachers should sincerely believe the gods of Homer/Hesiod rather than contradict the authorities they teach. It communicates a culturally exclusionary perception, not proof all schools followed the criteria uniformly.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Municipal `curiales` and imperial review are the legal gate; a philosopher's literary alignment might be considered. **No Alchemist title, alchemical school, chemical credential or identified rejected chrysopoeia expert** in examined texts.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Teaching can be policed institutionally; this does not establish covert alchemical manuals, later Christian pseudonyms, secret laboratories, or practical dangers motivating concealment.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`magistri studiorum doctoresque` denotes educators, not `chēmeutai`. The **17 June** date applies to CTh XIII.3.5, not automatically to Julian's **separate Greek letter**.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
Julian's political/religious school policy in **362** formally filters teacher status, offering a comparison for how a technical station might eventually become institutionally authorized.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
Legal approval system **prescribed**. Actual case of specific excluded or employed alchemical teacher **NO SECURE EVIDENCE**.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[CTh XIII.3.5 Latin](https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Constitutiones/CTh13.html); [Julian's explanatory Letter 36](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Works_of_the_Emperor_Julian/Letters/Letter_36); legal compilation 438 and later copied manuscripts.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
A formal civic/imperial **credentialing process for one intellectual station** is independently attested. It cannot be pasted onto an unrecorded alchemical profession.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
