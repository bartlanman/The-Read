# ST-094 — Nicomedia 335: An Actual Gold Coin Is Not Evidence of Transmutation

**Scope:** dated original material/monetary comparison, not a named practitioner. **Date:** 335 CE. **Original object:** Berlin Münzkabinett **18200650**, gold **solidus 4.54g, 22 mm**, RIC VII Nicomedia 179. **Grade:** ATTESTED minted existing gold and public imagery; NO SECURE EVIDENCE elemental chrysopoeia or Alchemist. [Window 27](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0321-0340_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Roman government struck precious-metal coinage as symbol and instrument of monetary political authority. Constantine's thirty-year jubilee informed the 335 type.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
Mintworkers struck existing gold into a measured coin showing imperial portrait and reverse Victory with `VOT XXX`. The object contains material gold; that does not prove a craftsman could **produce a new chemical element from another one**.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
`VICTORIA CONSTANTINI AVG` and thirty-year vow representation communicate imperial rule/victory. It is an official issuer's message, not a craftworker's claim of successful chrysopoeia.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
Named Constantine as issuer. Artisan's identity, training, workshop wages or independent Alchemist occupation not inscribed on coin.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
No recipe, guild oath, persecution motive, lab hazard, protected teacher lineage. Monetary value and original material are attested, secrecy not.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`VICTORIA CONSTANTINI AVG`, `VOT XXX`: political representation, not ingredients. Modern catalog's 'solidus' and 'gold' classify artifact and material, not alchemical procedure.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and date it entered
Imperial commemoration/economic authority in **335** shapes issuer/metal object. No change of station of named gold-making claimant observed.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
A commemorative coin was actually struck; no specific mint artisan's intellectual or religious response attested.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
[Berlin original specimen catalogue](https://ikmk.smb.museum/object?id=18200650), date **335** and RIC VII Nicomedia 179. Museum catalog modern; **physical coin ancient**.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
An actual gold object and public ruler's name **can coexist with absence of evidence** for the claimed technique of goldmaking. Actual material, skill, and public authority must be separated.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Source of bullion, precise worker method and identity, alleged chemical creation processes and any named Alchemist.
