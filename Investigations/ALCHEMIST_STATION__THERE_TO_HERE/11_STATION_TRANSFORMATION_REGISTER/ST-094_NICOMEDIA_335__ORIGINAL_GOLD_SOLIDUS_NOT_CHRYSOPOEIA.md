# ST-094 — 335 CE Nicomedia: A Genuine Gold Solidus Is Evidence of Minting, Not Gold Transmutation

**Scope:** original-period physical money and precious metalcraft comparator; **not** an Alchemist event. **Date of object:** **335 CE**. **Original witness:** [Berlin Münzkabinett 18200650](https://ikmk.smb.museum/object?id=18200650), solidus of Constantine at Nicomedia, **4.54 g**, **22 mm**, RIC VII Nicomedia 179; a related but distinct RIC type at [British Museum 1844,1015.310](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/C_1844-1015-310). **Grade:** **ATTESTED GOLD OBJECT**, **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** elemental creation or maker's alchemical status. [Window 27](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0321-0340_CE__WINDOW.md).

## THERE: surrounding conditions and existing station
Imperial monetary institutions routinely minted gold coinage and presented emperor's jubilee authority. The particular **Nicomedia** issue dated 335 is an original material counterpart to later legal list of specialized `aurifices`.

## CAPACITY: what the actor demonstrably could do
A coin was physically struck from **gold metal** with title, portrait and symbol; Berlin specimen mass/diameter and minted identification are actual. This documents handling and shaping of existing gold; it does **NOT** document extracting gold from ores by a particular route, identifying minter(s), or **transmutation from base metals**.

## PERCEPTION: what contemporaries believed or claimed
The inscriptions and `VOT / XXX` shield claim imperial political prestige and jubilee victory. Not a philosophical claim to having created gold from non-gold matter.

## AUTHORITY: who recognized, employed, patronized or challenged the actor
State issuer is named on minted coin; workers are **not personally named**. Gold coinage legitimacy is money/state authority, not a guild's technical competence license.

## SECRECY: institution / fear / economic value / competence / danger / prestige / loss
Nothing on the coin shows concealed chemistry, hazardous reagents, forced silence or an alchemical teacher. Mint technique presupposes skill but does not prove any special secrecy oath.

## LANGUAGE: exact terms, translation, technical idioms, pseudonyms, allegory, ambiguity
`VICTORIA CONSTANTINI AVG`, `VOT / XXX` represent imperial victory/vows associated with the reign's thirtieth year. They're **public political marks**, not coded ingredients. Labels `solidus`, `RIC VII 179` are numismatic descriptors.

## THE OUTSIDE: external pressure and the date it entered
Public monetary confidence and celebrating Constantine's reign are part of 335 minting; this original object cannot on its own identify shortages of metal, state policies on chemical expertise or impact of 337 exemptions.

## RESPONSE: changes, resistance, accommodation, continuity, or unverified
A dated original coin exists and survives. Any particular named mint worker's social or technical response to outside forces **UNVERIFIED**.

## TEXTS MANIFESTED: work, composition date, copy date, passage and authority claim
Original [Berlin coin 18200650](https://ikmk.smb.museum/object?id=18200650) and separate [British Museum 1844,1015.310](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/C_1844-1015-310). Not a surviving alchemical manuscript, not two witnesses to gold being artificially synthesized.

## HERE: what becomes newly warranted
A physical gold product gives direct evidence of **material skill and state money** but not of a specialized social authority to reinterpret matter. Proof of precious-metal object ≠ proof of the method ancient chrysopoeia claims.

## REMAINDER: unknowns, false bridges, contradictory evidence
Ore origin, complete gold-fabrication chaîne opératoire, alloy trace assays, actual artisans, separate teaching contracts, connection with Zosimos or alleged alchemy guild.
