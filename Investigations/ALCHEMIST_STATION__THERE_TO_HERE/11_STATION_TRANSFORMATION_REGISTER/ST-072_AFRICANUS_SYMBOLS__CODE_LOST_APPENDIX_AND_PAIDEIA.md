# ST-072 — The Missing Code Key: Geometrical and Musical Ingredient References

**Textual source:** Pieces of Africanus' `Kestoi` preserved in **later excerpts/fragments**, critically discussed in **Olivier Dufault (2019), pp.88–90**.
**Status:** **Deliberate symbolic substitution documented in textual tradition**; **purpose and how exclusive access was are NOT proven**.
**Material limit:** The British Library `P.Oxy III 412` Book 18 papyrus does **NOT** itself contain this notation.

## THERE
A Greek-educated author working around the Severan court transmits diverse useful and unusual procedures as a literary corpus.

## CAPACITY
Some recipe expressions substitute references to **geometrical figures and musical notes** for ordinarily named ingredients. Text points to an **appendix defining values**, but the appendix is not extant. Exact missing labels cannot be recovered without new witnesses.

## PERCEPTION / AUTHORITY
A reader could regard the notation as evidence of technical or literary sophistication; an educated reader might decode it with knowledge of Greek geometry/music. The same form can operate as **play, demonstration of `paideia`, group familiarity or restricted access** depending on actual historical audience.

## SECRECY — DISCRIMINATING THE MOTIVES
**Actual symbolic substitution:** source-supported.
**Effective encryption against educated Greek readers:** Dufault expressly considers **unlikely**.
**Learned puzzle or educational display:** Dufault proposes as plausible.
**Commercial exclusivity / persecuted authors / safety lock:** no independent testimony; **UNKNOWN, do not assert**.
**Lost key:** actual transmission gap. Modern obscurity **cannot** be projected backward as the intention.

## THE OUTSIDE
Patronage, scholarly competition and educational prestige may reward elaborate presentations. Changes in manuscript transmission can remove formerly available explanatory aids.

## HERE
**A writer may intentionally symbolize a technical instruction without wanting to conceal it from qualified contemporaries. The later loss of the reference key can transform a once-readable puzzle into an apparent secret.**

## REMAINDER
Original appendix, exact word–sign mappings, who had access, date of first sign usage, whether this component itself belonged to Africanus' original edition.
