# Window 08 — 60–41 BCE source register

**Review date:** 2026-10-18. **Method:** Compare transmitted Chinese primary text, its medieval annotations, contemporary Ciceronian/ Caesarian partisan testimony, dated Ptolemaic chronology, and modern scholarship on later alchemical Cleopatra. Do not count multiple copies of the same *Hanshu* as independent ancient witnesses.

## [W08-P01] *Hanshu* 36, Chu Yuanwang zhuan, biography of Liu Gengsheng / Xiang
- **Chinese annotated original:** https://zidian.donglishuzhai.net/chapter/858.html
- **Alternative accessible edited text:** https://www.wenlinshe.com/tw/chapter/zhuzibaijia/han-shu/han-shu-xin-zhu-juan-san-shi-liu-chu-yuan-wang-chuan-di-liu.html
- **Plain modern edition:** https://www.xuges.com/ls/hslz/018.htm
- **Source chronology:** First/second-century CE dynastic compilation about the mid-first-century BCE. Transmitted manuscript copies are later.
- **Key Chinese:** 上復興神僊方術之事，而淮南有枕中鴻寶苑祕書...世人莫見...更生幼而讀誦，以爲奇，獻之，言黃金可成。上令典尚方鑄作事，費甚多，方不驗。上乃下更生吏，吏劾更生鑄僞黃金，繫當死...其兄安民...贖罪...
- **Warrants:** reported rare written knowledge, Liu's childhood reading and claim, *Shangfang* royal workshop direction, costs, failure, counterfeit-gold legal accusation and family intervention.
- **Does not warrant:** surviving recipes, successful alchemical goldmaking, a stable guild of 'Alchemists', deliberate coded reagent lexicon, an exact year from the paragraph alone.
- **Dating:** c.60/c.56 BCE widely cited in secondary scholarship, but the primary biographical paragraph is not internally annalistic; treat years as CONVENTIONAL pending collation.

## [W08-P02] Historical commentaries on Han book title and Huainan family custody
- **Full annotated *Hanshu*:** https://zidian.donglishuzhai.net/chapter/858.html
- **Later Yan Shigu note:** 鴻寶苑祕書，並道術篇名。臧在枕中，言常存錄之不漏泄也 — treats Hongbao and Yuan-mi as names of technical/Daoist treatises, with guarded pillow custody.
- **Later source-critical note:** Liu Fengshi points out that a chronology assigning Liu De a role in the Huainan prosecution of 122 BCE clashes with his likely childhood age; this is a commentator's *objection* to the biography, not independently verified replacement narrative.
- **Warrant:** **textual and chronological uncertainty** in the asserted lineage, and explicit later reception of the book as guarded.
- **Limit:** medieval glosses are centuries later than the reported goldmaking; their interpretations are NOT statements from the 60s/50s BCE.

## [W08-S01] Fabrizio Pregadio, 'The Early History of Chinese Alchemy and the Way of the Great Clarity' (2006)
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.11126/stanford/9780804751773.003.0002
- **Warrant:** earliest Chinese gold/elixir tradition, Zhang/Yellow Emperor court contexts and mid-second-century *Hongbao* book attributed to Huainan.
- **Critical:** the rare text from the alleged earlier era was **lost**, so later *Hanshu* may preserve claims but no complete recipe; contemporary station evidence is for Xuan-era intervention.

## [W08-S02] Homer H. Dubs, 'The Beginnings of Alchemy', *Isis* 38 (1947), pp. 62–86
- **Bibliographic record:** https://www.jstor.org/stable/225498
- **Warrant:** influential older reconstruction of Liu Xiang's attempt (conventional 60/56 BCE dates), as repeated in later scholarship.
- **Limit:** some broader Dubs claims (e.g. treating Jingdi 144 BCE anti-counterfeiting law as an anti-alchemy prohibition) have been rejected by Pregadio; do not adopt all of Dubs' theoretical chronology uncritically.

## [W08-P03] Cicero, *Pro Rabirio Postumo* 25–29, delivered 54 BCE
- **Latin and English translation:** https://www.attalus.org/cicero/rab_post.html
- **Alternative edition:** https://cicero.hermitsh.ai/en/works/pro-rabirio-postumo/
- **Warrant:** Roman creditor becomes **dioikētēs** (royal treasurer/revenue administrator), title and garments an acknowledged requirement; Ptolemaic dependence on outside credit and its social effect.
- **Limit:** contemporary but partisan defense speech; do not take claims of coercion/innocence uncritically. This is finance administration, not alchemy.

## [W08-P04] Caesar, *Bellum civile* 3.103, 107–108, Alexandrian dispute 48 BCE
- **Loeb English:** https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Caesar/Civil_Wars/3D%2A.html
- **Warrants:** Caesar explicitly asserts Roman right to adjudicate Cleopatra/Ptolemy XIII dispute, based on Senate/legal relations; recounts copies of Ptolemy XII's will in Rome and Alexandria.
- **Limit:** Caesar's own justification is partisan and does not prove his jurisdiction was uncontested or explain all later regime outcome. The will's text is not a preserved technical manual.

## [W08-E01] UCL Digital Egypt — Ptolemy XII
- **URL:** https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/chronology/ptolemyxii.html
- **Dates:** 80–51 BCE reign, **interrupted 58–55 BCE** by Berenice IV; temple work and royal representation.
- **Limit:** no evidence of a court alchemist or decoded chemical text.

## [W08-E02] Oxford Classical Dictionary, 'Cleopatra VII, 69–30 BCE'
- **URL:** https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/548065820
- **Use:** co-regency 52–51, younger brother, Caesar 48–47, subsequent relationships, uncertainties in Roman framing.
- **Limit:** Queen's scientific authorship not established by the chronology.

## [W08-E03] American Research Center in Egypt — Marina Escolano-Poveda, 'Cleopatra VII: Scholar, Patron, Queen'
- **URL:** https://arce.org/resource/cleopatra-vii-scholar-patron-queen/
- **Use:** queen's political station; reconstructs later different strand of Cleopatra-as-scholar/alchemist and explores possible connection to queen's self-presentation.
- **Caution:** possible identification is not proof of direct queen authorship; *Marcianus gr.299* dates tenth/eleventh c CE; ancient dialogue contains anachronistic figures.

## [W08-S03] Virginia Fabrizi, 'Cleopatra, alchemist', *Encyclopedia of Ancient History* (online first 2019; current citation 2026)
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1002/9781444338386.wbeah21086.pub2
- **Use:** alchemical writings 'Cleopatra' of later Greek/Byzantine tradition likely spurious, show authority assigned to queen's name. Death/rebirth metaphor in metal transformation.
- **Important disagreement:** ARCE author explores a possible remembered queen connection; Fabrizi says works very probably spurious. Record **CONTESTED IDENTIFICATION**, no contemporaneous authored work assigned.

## [W08-E04] Cambridge University Press 2026 excerpt, Sally-Ann Ashton, *Profiling Cleopatra*
- **PDF excerpt:** https://assets.cambridge.org/97810094/87788/excerpt/9781009487788_excerpt.pdf
- **Accessible text includes table** dates 59 BCE Caesar recognizes Ptolemy XII; 58 exile; 55 return with Roman army; 51 succession.
- **Note:** for any interpretation of the excerpt based on page imagery, verify printed pages/figures; this register uses extracted text only.

## [W08-E05] Dorothy J. Thompson, 'Egypt 146–31 BC', *Cambridge Ancient History*, 2nd ed. (1994)
- **Text excerpt:** https://pressbooks.claremont.edu/clas112pomonavalentine/chapter/thompson-d-1994-egypt-146-31-b-c-in-j-crook-a-lintott-e-rawson-eds-the-cambridge-ancient-history-the-cambridge-ancient-history-pp-310-326-cambridge-cambridge-univer/
- **Warrant:** crises in Egyptian grain harvest 51–48 BCE, 50 BCE edict directing grain to Alexandria, Roman financial debts exacerbating economic crisis.
- **Limit:** records of food shortages and debt do not prove alchemical workshop creation or suppression.

## [W08-S04] ARCE / Fabrizi comparative alchemical text reception (see E03, S03)
- **Chrysopoeia:** 10th/11th c MS Venice Marcianus gr. Z.299 folio; older attribution may invoke Cleopatra VII, but original author, date, technical claims and transmission remain unverified for this window.
- **Do not collapse:** Cleopatra VII, Cleopatra the Alchemist / Wise, Cleopatra the Physician. The later literary use of a historical woman's name can itself construct authority.

## Essential open research tasks
1. Confirm precise date of Liu Xiang's court sponsorship and reprieve in regnal annals; avoid absolutizing c.60/c.56.
2. Collate Yan Shigu and Liu Fengshi on two-book vs single-book segmentation and Huainan father-story, and map downstream manuscripts.
3. Determine evidence for Shangfang's metallurgy facilities in Xuandi's court, what quantity was spent, and whether any physical products survive.
4. Collect papyri, coins and temple inscriptions dated 58–41 BCE to identify which Egyptian fiscal/ritual changes affected actual workshops.
5. Distinguish authentic queen Cleopatra documents from later alchemical Cleopatra authorial attributions in later dedicated 3rd–11th-century windows.
