# 101–120 CE — Source Register (Window 16)

**Backfilled 2026-10-18 following an interrupted previous window deposit.** Source weights: surviving Roman brick stamps, Greek papyri and later source-critical reconstructions of individual identity. **No period-dated Alchemist practitioner attested.**

## [W16-B01] Oxford Classical Dictionary, Roman brick stamping
https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/548526368
- **CIL XV 18**, first consular-dated brick stamp **in Rome's urban industry sequence** from `figlinae Brutianae`, owned by **M. Rutilius Lupus**, **110 CE**.
- This is NOT first dated brick anywhere. The stamp names provenance/owner, not necessarily person who molded clay.
## [W16-B02] Ashmolean University of Oxford Latin Non-monumental catalogue
https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/arts/classics/research/dept_projects/latininscriptions/resources/ashli.finalcatalogue.nonmonumental.pdf
- Records a surviving **116 CE** consular-dated brick stamp from Lupus-associated `figlinae Brutianae`, a second physical manufacturing anchor in this window.
- Identifies M. Rutilius Lupus with prefect of grain c.107–112 and prefect of Egypt 113–117, prosopography PIR² R252.
## [W16-P01] P.Brem.4, Greek papyrus (TM 19589)
https://egyptianinscriptions.org/object/127603
- **113–117 CE**, period papyrus; Apollonios strategos writes to **Rutilius Lupus** prefect regarding co-heirs / inheritance disputes.
- Original Greek `Ῥουτιλίωι Λούπωι τῶι κρατίστωι ἡγεμόνι`. Do not use AI-generated English as philological proof.
- A petition about property, not an alchemical manual or necessarily a revolt letter.
## [W16-P02] Apollonios archive project and P.Brem.7 family letter
https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/research-radar/10.55776/P29164
https://brema.suub.uni-bremen.de/papyri/content/titleinfo/770778
- c.113–120 CE ~230 Greek administrative/private papyri; c.116 (inferred) Heras letter on relocation of family from danger around Hermopolis.
## [W16-H01] Miriam Pucci Ben Zeev, Cambridge History of Judaism, 116–117 uprising
https://doi.org/10.1017/CHOL9780521772488.005
- Earlier anti-Jewish attacks in 112 and summer 115, then wider war/uprising and violent Roman suppression 116–117. Political causation disputed; preserve actual Jewish suffering; never infer an alchemical purge.
## [W16-E01] Getty, Benevento obelisks 88/89 CE
https://www.getty.edu/publications/egypt-classical-world/07/
- **Rutilius Lupus** patron identified on period original. Identifying him with **Marcus** later brickworks proprietor/prefect **is a proposed but unresolved identification** due to homonyms.
## [W16-E02] Trajan's kiosk Philae
https://echoesofegypt.peabody.yale.edu/overview/campfire-river-kiosk-trajan-philae
- Trajan (98–117 CE) relief depiction, structural building date may predate him. Ancient Egyptian image ≠ chemistry text.
## [W16-G01] Dufault, *Early Greek Alchemy* (2019)
https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2ks0g83x
- Maria primarily via later Zosimos (~300 CE), not dated original 101–120 text. No biographical link to revolt documented.
