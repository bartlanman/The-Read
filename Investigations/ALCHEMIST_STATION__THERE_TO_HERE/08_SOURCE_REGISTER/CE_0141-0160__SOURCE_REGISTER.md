# Window 18 — 141–160 CE historical source register

**Prepared 2026-10-18.** **Evidence ranking:** actual 159/160 priest papyrus and 154/155 workshop lease > dated Roman-Egyptian material coin > reconstructed Galen career from self-authored later texts > Greek alchemical works whose original composition is unsettled.

## [W18-P02] Berlin Papyrus Collection, BGU I 16 (P. 6889), 159/160 CE
https://berlpap.smb.museum/eidliche-erklaerung/?lang=en
- Original Greek papyrus, **15 preserved lines**, from **Soknopaiou Nesos**, Fayum. **23rd year of Antoninus Pius**, **30 Aug 159–28 Aug 160 CE**.
- Accusation: Pasis son of Neilos charges **priest Panephremmis** with unshaven hair and woolen garments, violating regulated Egyptian cult habitus.
- **Five senior priests of temple of Soknopaios** give sworn statement requested via **strategos Hierax** and **royal scribe Teimagenes**, after dossier/appeal before **Idios Logos** office.
- Berlin curator mentions a **1,000 drachma fine** associated with accusation; **papyrus fragment BREAKS OFF after oath before full declaration**, so **end result of investigation is unknown**. Do not claim proof of final payment or confirmation of guilt.
- **Not evidence:** chemical expertise, hidden transmutational procedure, official alchemist ban, persecution as an alchemist.

## [W18-P01] P.Tebt. II 613 (TM 20821), Lease of a House with Workshops, Antinoopolis
https://egyptianinscriptions.org/object/110155
- HGV catalog says **155 CE**, tied to **Antoninus Pius regnal year 18 (154/155 CE)**; another linked record gives 154/155.
- The translated lease includes **house with appurtenances and workshops**, quarter **Gamma**, rent **100 drachmas** yearly according to partly fragmentary record.
- It does **not** name what was manufactured in the workshops, whether laboratory, dye work, masonry, metal, textiles, etc. Do not invent a trade.
- Artifact is ancient Greek lease, viewed here through papyrological metadata and scholarly translation. Egyptian Inscriptions site adds machine-generated image notes; **ignore** those for source authority.

## [W18-O01] Roman Provincial Coinage, Oxford/Ashmolean, RPC IV.4 787
https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/coins/4.4/787
- **144/145 CE** Roman-Egyptian coin dated **year 8** (`L Η`) of **Antoninus Pius**; Alexandria mint, **copper-based alloy**, **Menelaites nome** reverse depicting **Harpocrates with crocodile lower body**.
- Names/ruler, date, iconographic public combination are physical.
- Note date **144/145** spans Egyptian regnal-year change; don't pick unearned single day.
- The iconography does not license a claim the mint or priesthood possessed occult Egyptian metallurgy.

## [W18-G01] *Oxford Handbook of Galen* chronology (2024)
https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/57517/chapter/467334321
https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780190913687.002.0008
- **143** philosophic studies; **145–148** first medical training under **Satyrus/Pelops** (school of **Quintus**); **148–157** study/travel (Smyrna, Corinth ~151, Alexandria ~153); **157** formal gladiator physician appointment at Pergamum.
- **Crucial note by editors:** Galen's early life is **mainly attested by Galen's own writings**; many dates reconstructed and early works later revised.
- **162–166** public anatomical demonstrations in Rome are **FUTURE**, not within 141–160.

## [W18-G02] Boudon-Millot, 'Galen: Life and Works', Oxford Handbook (2024)
https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/57517/chapter-abstract/467335083
- Galen writes medicine and philosophy, has clinical career and receives specialist instruction in Smyrna/Corinth/Alexandria, then gladiator-physician post at Pergamum.
- Later Galenic self-publicizing clinical claims and texts need evaluation rather than automatically converting literary success into independently validated experimental results.

## [W18-G03] Stanley et al., historical Galen overview (2014)
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3991212/
- Secondary medical history confirms c.157 gladiator work and Alexandrian training in broad outline.
- Dependence on ancient Galen and other received biographies possible; this is not a direct contemporaneous job letter or register.

## [W18-G04] Galen, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
https://plato.sydney.edu.au/entries/galen/
- Academic historical background on Galen's philosophy, scientific method and medical career; no warrant to label him an Alchemist or attribute his later pharmacology in fully developed form to **157 CE**.

## [W18-A01] Greek alchemy critical dating guard
https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/alchemy-reader/pseudodemocritus-first-or-second-century-ad-from-the-treatise-of-democritus-on-things-natural-and-mystical/B00116659657F0C20F64FBC1017E997B
https://www.routledge.com/The-Four-Books-of-Pseudo-Democritus/Martelli/p/book/9781909662285
- Cambridge anthology describes pseudo-Democritus **first or second century** with uncertain origin; Martelli favors broadly **first century, possibly later first century**. Manuscript/author dating contested. Original lost.
- Cannot select 150 CE just because the broad date range covers it.

## [W18-A02] Maria the Jewess, chronology caution
https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt7zvb6p.11
https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2ks0g83x
- Attested primarily by later alchemical writers including Zosimos around 300 CE; her own original documents, training and date unknown. No dateable 141–160 contemporaneous activity established.
