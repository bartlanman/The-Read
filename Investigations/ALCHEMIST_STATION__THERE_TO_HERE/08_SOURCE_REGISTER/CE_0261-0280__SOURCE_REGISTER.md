# Window 24 — Source and Historical Witness Register, 261–280 CE

**Research pass:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** primary-object/catalographic cross-check with explicit source-date controls. **Claim grades:** ATTESTED (surviving ancient objects or dated writing); RETROSPECTIVE (later testimony); SOURCE-SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION; CONTESTED; NO SECURE EVIDENCE. Coin *type catalogs* aggregate multiple original specimens: multiple entries for one coin do not mean independent historical texts, and specimens of one type are not distinct episodes.

## [W24-P01] British Library Papyrus 2040 — P.Oxy. III 412 (recto); P.Oxy. VI 907 (verso)

- **Primary museum object/catalog:** https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002105408 (BL shelfmark Papyrus 2040).
- **Independent scholarly manuscript metadata:** https://www.coptic-magic.phil.uni-wuerzburg.de/index.php/manuscript/kyp-m759/ (Kyprianos M759; uses siglum PGM XXIII / P.Oxy III 412); dates **recto broadly 225–275**, later reuse **25 June–24 July 276** (a dated Egyptian month window, **not proof that every event in the will was performed that day**). BL gives overarching **225–276**.
- **RECTO (original manuscript, palaeographically dated):** part/end of **Sextus Julius Africanus, Kestoi Book 18**, columns numbered 35 and 36, with Odyssey/Homeric necromancy, discussion of authenticity and book title; literary / learned textual circulation. `P.Oxy III 412` = `P.Lond.Lit 174`, Trismegistos **TM 61406**.
- **VERSO (original documentary papyrus, dated 276):** **P.Oxy VI 907**, `TM 20370`, **copy of a will** for **Hermogenes alias Eudaemon**, **councillor and officeholder** at Oxyrhynchus. He apportions real property, village holdings, urban house, enslaved persons and family arrangements among wife and **five children** (three sons and two daughters); provides dowries, guardianship and a bequest. The manuscript ends with a statement of opening date.
- **Material order:** Earlier `Kestoi` text along papyrus fibers → later document **across fibers and upside down** on reverse. Manuscript sheet/roll reused. Two distinct text acts, **one physical papyrus**. **The legal will is securely in Window 24**; its earlier literary copy could have been written any time in **225–275**; **cannot identify a 276 Kestoi author, reader or operative recipe**.
- **Important:** This **Book 18 recto** is NOT the separate `Kestoi` recipe fragments that cite musical/geometric substituted ingredient names. There is **no encoded dye recipe physically visible in these two columns**. See prior [Window 22 source register](CE_0221-0240__SOURCE_REGISTER.md) and [ST-071](../11_STATION_TRANSFORMATION_REGISTER/ST-071_KETOI_BOOK_18_OXYRHYNCHUS__PAPYRUS_AND_276_WILL.md).
- **Modern discussions:** [British Library catalog reference list](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002105408), esp. R.S. Bagnall, “An Owner of Literary Papyri,” *Classical Philology* **87** (1992), 137–140. Associations between literary manuscript ownership and a named family are **scholarly reconstructions**, not a colophon naming a third-century Alchemist. Current audit does not inspect Bagnall's full text; exact identity of original roll owner remains **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Analytic role:** secure **legal office, inheritance, material reuse** and **dated later terminus** for a written artifact, *not* an alchemical guild. [ST-081]

## [W24-O01] Aurelian/Vaballathus joint coin — Alexandria AD 270/271

- **Actual museum specimen:** https://hcr.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/coin/hcr31960 (**Ashmolean HCR31960**, former Milne 4304); source links to Roman Provincial Coinage X type **ID 75751** https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/75751 .
- **Object:** struck **billon** Alexandrian tetradrachm, **8.98 g**, **21.8 mm**, dating **270/271**. Aurelian **obverse** laureate with Greek title and `L A` (year **1**); Vaballathus **reverse** laureate with Greek titulature (some members of the type with `L Δ`, his year **4**). **Use particular specimen's visible/recorded inscription rather than infer each printed letter from generic coin types**.
- **Separate material comparators:** HCR31972, HCR31973 are specimens of same RPC ID 75751, not independent political events.
- **What it warrants:** a single struck coin can publicly put **two named rulers on opposed faces**, with asymmetric titulature/relative rank. Not proof of personal co-equal acceptance, individual artisan identity, a precious metal conversion or a magical dual union. [ST-079]

## [W24-O02] Zenobia Augusta coin — Alexandria, 272 CE

- **RPC cataloged ancient types:** https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/75758 (**RPC X ID 75758**, Zenobia/Homonoia); https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/75756 (Zenobia/Elpis); https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/75761 (Zenobia/Selene). All represent Alexandrian **272 CE** issues, **year five `L E` of Vaballathus**, with Zenobia's title **`Σεπτ(ιμία) Ζηνοβία Σεβαστή`** (Augusta).
- **Actual museum specimen:** https://hcr.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/coin/hcr32033 (**Ashmolean HCR32033**, draped diademed Zenobia, reverse **Homonoia**, recorded `L Δ`; contrast the RPC type listing `L E`). **IMPORTANT CATALOG VARIANCE:** HCR32033's illustrated/recorded reverse date **`L Δ`** and RPC type 75758's **`L E`** differ. **Do not silently reconcile** or call HCR32033 necessarily a type 75758 specimen. Prefer the RPC 75758 published type for year-five example; treat HCR32033 as a separately cataloged specimen **requiring die/inscription review**, not proof same date-sign. This is a documented metadata discrepancy, not an invented additional year.
- **Interpretation:** `Σεβαστή` is a publicly minted sovereignty claim; **Homonoia** means Concord/unity as personification on a coin, NOT proof of peaceful concord in the region or alchemical fusion. The metal and the text retain **disputable political meaning**. [ST-080]

## [W24-O03] Aurelian-alone Alexandria coin after recovery — 273/274

- **Actual specimen:** https://hcr.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/coin/hcr31900 , **Ashmolean HCR31900**, Alexandria struck billon tetradrachm, **10.72 g**, **20.8 mm**, dated **273/274**; `Α Κ Λ ΔΟΜ ΑΥΡΗΛΙΑΝΟϹ ϹΕΒ` obverse; `L E` (year five) with Zeus head and star reverse. RPC X type **75719** https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/type/75719.
- **What it warrants:** Aurelian issued money without co-imaging Palmyrene rulers by **273/274**, despite the continuity of **minted billon coin competence**.
- **NOT warranted:** exact staff continuity, uninterrupted functioning every day of the siege, an alchemical change to alloy, or evidence of the library's fate. [ST-080]

## [W24-S03] Robert Suski (2018), “Aurelian and the Library of Alexandria. A History of a Certain Misunderstanding”

- Peer-reviewed historical paper abstract https://bibliotekarzpodlaski.pl/index.php/bp/article/view/131 ; **DOI 10.36770/bp.131**.
- Argues dating the complete destruction of Alexandria's famous library to Aurelian's military struggle is **unsupported by adequately secure ancient testimony**; Ammianus's topography/history is problematic, and the actual timing/cause remains undetermined. Original article PDF is in Polish; **this English abstract provides claim-level support only**, not independent close reading of the entire paper.
- **CONTESTED attribution / NO SECURE link** to 272 alchemical manuscript destruction. Treat this as **negative historiographic control**, not proof library remained intact. [ST-080]

## [W24-S04] Zosimos/Maria and relative Greek alchemical genealogy

- Olivier Dufault (2019), *Early Greek Alchemy, Patronage and Innovation in Late Antiquity*: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2ks0g83x .
- Michèle Mertens, “Zosimus, alchemist” in *Encyclopedia of Ancient History*, published 2012: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781444338386.wbeah21346 ; Zosimos **floruit c.300**, written late third / early fourth centuries.
- Cambridge/Stanton J. Linden *The Alchemy Reader*, ch.7, “Zosimos of Panopolis (fl. c.300 AD)”: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/alchemy-reader/zosimos-of-panopolis-fl-c-300-ad-of-virtue-lessons-13/0FD98882E6321A144B49317EFDB33647 .
- Physical Leiden X / Stockholm recipe papyri: approximate late third/fourth-century manuscript horizon; **no securely assignable copying in 261–280**. Their recipe content may be older than extant papyrus but not provably 275.
- For Mary/Maria the Jewess, works attributed in later compendia do not securely date her birth, death, a 275 public occupation, or named apprentices. **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** of a named **261–280** Alchemist.

## [W24-S05] Berlin State Museums numismatic explanation: Aurelian's 274 monetary reform and marks

- Original 270–272 Antioch coin with Aurelian/Vaballathus: https://ikmk.smb.museum/object?id=18257648 ; museum explanation notes that the silver content of Roman double-denarius coinage declined sharply, Aurelian's **c.274** currency reform and the **`XXI` / `KA`** marks have **disputed interpretation** (denominational numeral versus metallic fineness ratio).
- **Terminology trap:** the term “Antoninianus” itself is a **modern naming convention**, not a securely attested ancient coin name. `XXI` cannot be used as a proven alchemical alloy recipe without testing controversy. **No 274 chemical gold production established**.
- [Window 24 language dossier](../09_LANGUAGE_TECHNIQUE_AND_ALLEGORY/CE_0261-0280__METAL_AUTHORITY_AND_REUSED_TEXT.md) preserves alternative readings and actual object vs catalogue level.

## Source-critical and future audit

- **Explicit no-found claim:** This identified original-period sample has named *rulers* and *law-office holder*, **not** a named 261–280 Greek practitioner titled or commissioned to make transmuted gold.
- No independently dateable workshop crucible, Egyptian artisan oath, alchemical school register or chemical formula from **261–280** established in this pass.
- More complete Oxyrhynchus / Trismegistos full Greek transcription of **P.Oxy VI 907** remains to inspect; this dossier relies on BL catalogue plus Würzburg and original scholarly edition references, not a claim to have personally read the complete diplomatic will.
- More evidence may change the assessment; preservation/non-preservation is not a proof of historical nonexistence.
