## Evidence hierarchy
1. Dated contemporary publication, instrument, law, institutional rule, appointment, appointment denial, correspondence, or experimental report.
2. Later chronicle / retrospective participant narrative.
3. Modern critical interpretation (explicitly named as such).
4. Our THERE → HERE hypothesis (not evidence).

## Essential caveats
- *Pseudoscience* is a judgment of scientific status in a later context, **not** an historically available first-century Roman Egyptian category.
- Separate material impossibility of chemical transmutation of one element into another from the possible **physical** transformation of atomic nuclei.
- The 1919 nuclear experiment is not evidence that any medieval alchemical process worked. Do not attribute to Rutherford in 1919 a more detailed interpretation established only later.
- Criticism of fraudulent transmuters does not imply every alchemical experiment was a fraud.
- Modern historiography's rehabilitation of alchemy's place in history is **not** validation of historically impossible chemical pathways to gold.
- Lack of a published procedure can reflect confidentiality, limited readership, training, or missing texts; do not infer deliberate persecution.
