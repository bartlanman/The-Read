# Window 09 source register — 40–21 BCE: conquered station, multilingual political writing and Liu Xiang's return

**Method:** dated physical inscription, Greek historical text with later writing/revision, Chinese dynastic history compiled later, and modern specialist work. **Never assign source composition to the historical date it describes.** Checked 2026-10-18.

## [W09-R01] Augustus, *Res Gestae* 27 — Egypt annexed
- Historical event: 30 BCE; retrospective first-person imperial self-presentation written late in Augustus' life, published posthumously in 14 CE.
- Latin and English: https://www.livius.org/sources/content/augustus-res-gestae/ and https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/e/roman/texts/augustus/res_gestae/5%2A.html
- Warrant: self-claim to incorporate Egypt as Roman territory (Aegyptum imperio populi Romani adieci).
- Limit: text's assertion of annexation is not evidence all temple or educational stations ended.

## [W09-R02] Friederike Herklotz, 'Aegypto Capta: Augustus and the Annexation of Egypt' (2012)
- *Oxford Handbook of Roman Egypt*, pp. 11–21. DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780199571451.013.0002
- Warrant: 30 BCE imperial annexation, Roman prefect replacing monarchy, Augustus cultivating priestly relationships and rebuilding temples.
- Limit: general region-wide persistence is not proof of one concrete gold-making workshop's continued operation.

## [W09-P01] Cornelius Gallus, trilingual stele from Philae (29 BCE)
- **Material witness dated 29 BCE:** OGIS 654 = CIL III 14147.5 = IG Philae II 128.
- Translated inscription with notes: https://attalus.org/docs/ogis/s654.html
- Specialist critical 2009 edition / University Trier project: https://www.uni-trier.de/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iii/faecher/aegyptologie/forschung/projekte/stele-des-cornelius-gallus
- Bibliography of scholarly edition: Friedhelm Hoffmann, Martina Minas-Nerpel, Stefan Pfeiffer, *Die dreisprachige Stele des C. Cornelius Gallus* (de Gruyter 2009). Research record https://cronfa.swan.ac.uk/Record/cronfa11490
- Warrant: first Roman prefect publicly legitimates acts via **Latin, Greek and hieroglyphic Egyptian**; Egyptian portion differs from Latin and Greek. It is not evidence of deliberate formula encryption.
- Limit: full word-by-word textual collation should use 2009 edition, not just summary; historical self-presentation is partisan.

## [W09-R04] Cassius Dio 53.23 — Gallus disgrace
- Primary Greek Roman retrospective history written in third century CE; online English: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Cassius_Dio/53%2A.html
- Date uncertainty and ancient source comparison: Durham guide https://livingpoets.dur.ac.uk/w/index.php/Gallus%3A_A_Guide_to_Selected_Sources
- Warrant: story of Roman prefect's self-advertisement and political fall; suicide placed at **27 or 26 BCE** by different witnesses.
- Limit: later moralizing prose not immediate court hearing minutes; don't convert into alchemy prosecution.

## [W09-P02] Strabo, *Geography*, Book 17.1.8 and 17.1.29
- Visited Egypt **about 25–24 BCE** with prefect **Aelius Gallus**, distinct person from Cornelius Gallus. Authored/revised geography **later**, largely first century CE.
- Text 17.1.8: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Strabo/17A1%2A.html
- Text 17.1.29: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Strabo/17A3%2A.html
- Greek text specific passage: https://reader.humanitext.ai/en/read/urn%3Acts%3AgreekLit%3Atlg0099.tlg001.humanitext-grc2%3A17.1.29-17.1.31
- Warrant, 17.1.8: office of **Mouseion priest** now appointed by Caesar/Augustus, whereas kings appointed it formerly. Direct account of the same named role surviving change in patron.
- Warrant, 17.1.29: Strabo *claims* to have visited Heliopolis and found only officiating/explaining priests rather than the earlier scholarly astronomy school; **he reports a story** about earlier learned priests who withheld much astronomy from Greek students, while transmitting some through translation.
- Greek cues (check primary edition): μυστικούς ('secretive'), δυσμεταδότους ('slow/reluctant to impart'), ἀπεκρύψαντο ('hid/concealed').
- Limit: original years of alleged Plato/Eudoxus schooling unclear, account's source identity contested; no inference that Roman annexation caused decline; no recorded alchemical laboratory.

## [W09-R05] Hugh Lindsay, 'Strabo and Augustan Egypt' (2023)
- https://doi.org/10.1080/00397679.2022.2128560
- Warrant: Strabo's residence in Alexandria in 20s BCE and a pro-imperial literary filter, economic interpretation and portrayal of priests and local tradition. His later writing contains both autopsy and inherited material.
- Limit: different passage types demand distinct source criticism.

## [W09-H01] *Hanshu*, Chengdi ji (Emperor Cheng annals), Heping year 3, autumn month eight
- Primary historical testimony **date: 26 BCE**, compilation first/second-century CE.
- Relevant line: 河平三年秋八月乙卯，光祿大夫劉向校中祕書，謁者陳農使，使求遺書於天下.
- Reproduced original and source identifications: https://www.shidianguji.com/book/GJTS21/chapter/1lp6esbibkizq
- Secondary text chronology: https://m.cidianwang.com/lishi/shijian/8/438nc.htm
- Warrant: dated state book-recovery and editorial assignment for Liu Xiang.
- Limit: not evidence Liu proved goldmaking or the secret Huainan recipe was in the acquired corpus.

## [W09-H02] *Hanshu*, chapter 30, *Yiwenzhi* Bibliographic Treatise
- Primary Chinese text: https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=64404&remap=gb&show=parallel
- Full convenient text: https://chinesebook.org/books/han-shu/chapter/han-shu-30
- Passage: 诏光禄大夫刘向校经传诸子诗赋，步兵校尉任宏校兵书，太史令尹咸校数术，侍医李柱国校方技.
- Warrant: four editorial stations for categories: Liu Xiang on classics/philosophy/verse; Ren Hong on war books; Yin Xian on numbers/calculation/divination; Li Zhuguo on *fangji* technical/medical arts. The treatise also reports textual summaries and eventual Liu Xin *Qilue*, both later developments.
- Limit: 26 BCE edict is a starting date for project, NOT the finishing date of *Bielu/Qilue*; portions of the treatise were transmitted through later centuries.
- Important: historical categorization of technical arts is not itself a laboratory credential.

## [W09-S01] Modern textual scholarship on Liu Xiang / Liu Xin editing
- Example: Wu Yiyun, '刘向、刘歆校书问题考述' with *Hanshu* passages: https://www.sohu.com/a/436494166_670305
- Warrant: cross-reference between dated Chengdi annals and *Yiwenzhi*; careful division of duties.
- Limit: actual text of book catalog not equivalent to original technical recipes.
