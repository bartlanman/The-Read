# Method and evidence

## Core THERE → HERE test

Every 20-year window must answer:
1. What historical **conditions** are demonstrably present?
2. What acts or material practices were **possible**?
3. What did observers and authors **perceive or claim** those acts meant?
4. What social **station**, if any, can be documented for practitioners?
5. Which persons, institutions, and **texts** are actually attested?
6. What **changed** relative to the previous window?
7. What is **missing or contested**?

## Evidence labels

- **ATTESTED** — supported by datable primary evidence; cite object, passage, and edition.
- **BROADLY DATED** — date range exceeds one window; link to *every plausible* window without inventing an exact year.
- **CONTESTED** — date, attribution, event, or interpretation subject to serious dispute; record competing views.
- **INTERPRETATION** — our explicitly flagged THERE → HERE inference.
- **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** — no evidence found to assign to this interval; never assert that nothing happened.

## Dating discipline

Separate event date, composition date, manuscript copying date, first citation, later attribution, and modern scholarly dating. Record BCE/CE without a year zero. An author's pseudonym, legendary identity, or later label must not be treated as direct evidence for their lifetime or profession.

## Distinct senses of 'station'

- **Capability:** someone can perform specialized material transformations.
- **Knowledge claim:** someone presents principles and interprets prior practice.
- **Recognized social role:** patrons, readers, institutions, or rivals treat that person as a specialist.
- **Named category:** surviving sources provide the relevant historical appellation; record the original language and context.

Do not collapse these.
