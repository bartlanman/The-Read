# The secrecy audit — protocol for every chronological window

Use with [the central question](../03_PERCEPTION_AND_STATION/SECRECY__COMPETING_CAUSES.md) and the [20-year template](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/WINDOW_TEMPLATE.md).

## Mandatory distinctions
1. **Institutional control** vs. **unauthorized practitioner's self-protection**.
2. **Market or political value** vs. **the time and training needed to attain competence**.
3. **Genuine chemical and practical dangers** vs. **claimed moral, ritual or spiritual dangers**.
4. **Secrecy as protection of existing expertise** vs. **secrecy as performance/creation of authority**.
5. **Intentional concealment** vs. **modern difficulty due to copying loss, translation or tacit knowhow**.
6. **Contemporary witness** vs. **later story about an earlier past**.

## Mandatory assessment per window
- Who could know the technique? Who could perform it? Who could *legitimately* perform it?
- Who was excluded, and who decided?
- Evidence of actual rule, oath, apprentice selection, economic benefit, danger, threatened penalty, or exposure?
- What was fully published? What was left unsaid?
- Does the evidence show the creation, maintenance, or contestation of a particular station?
- Can any cited item be securely dated to the window or is it only retrospective?

For each category use **ATTESTED, BROADLY DATED, CONTESTED, INTERPRETATION, NO SECURE EVIDENCE**. Do not award a positive secrecy label merely because a source is silent.
