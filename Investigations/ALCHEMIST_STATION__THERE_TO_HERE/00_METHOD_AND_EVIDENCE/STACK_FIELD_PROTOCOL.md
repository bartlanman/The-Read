# Nine-layer window protocol — THERE → HERE

**Every 20-year interval receives all nine layers.** A date range broader than 20 years must appear as a cross-window *candidate*, never as a precise event. Maintain chronological order without year zero.

1. **THERE — conditions**: politics, material infrastructure, economies, temples, crafts, languages, literacies, scholars.
2. **Technique**: actual materials, observable operations, vessels, apparatus, recipes; separate experiments from legend.
3. **Language/allegory**: historically attested lexemes and transformations; translation and copying can create opacity without intention.
4. **Secrecy/access**: institutional rule, protection from persecution, economic exclusivity, tacit skill, actual/claimed danger, prestige, accident of textual loss. Mark each independently.
5. **Perception**: what an observer or author took the act to mean; who was the audience?
6. **Station**: public office, paying patronage, teaching competence, professional title, community recognition; do not conflate.
7. **Lineage**: link by documented person-to-person contact, cited authority, passage of manuscripts, translation, later legendary attribution, or no evidence.
8. **Texts**: genre, alleged author, linguistic witness, probable composition range, extant manuscript date, attribution, interpretations; every text's *station effect*.
9. **HERE + remainder**: only changes warranted by explicit evidence, plus the unresolved differences and counterexamples.

## Epistemic status
- **DATED EVENT** belongs to one interval only.
- **BROADLY DATED** may touch many intervals without becoming multiple independent events.
- **CONTESTED** must name the disagreement and an alternative reading.
- **INTERPRETATION** is a transparent research hypothesis.
- **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** means no securely assignable record in this review, never impossibility or historical nonexistence.

## Disciplines
- A priestly text is not therefore an alchemical text.
- A chemical word with sacred resonance is not thereby proved deliberately encrypted.
- Narrative persecution reported centuries later is not a contemporary law.
- A substance can be toxic without danger having motivated its secrecy.
- A name like Democritus, Hermes or Geber can be pseudonymous and cannot function as a literal teacher/student link.
- Treat Chinese material as a parallel tradition, not a link of direct Greek transmission absent evidence.
- Do not treat every 20-year container as a claim that change happened there.

## Completion grades
**FIRST PASS** = a research container with scholarly anchor/caveats, **SOURCED DETAIL** = verified primary/witness close reading, **CROSSING VERIFIED** = station transition demonstrated by multiple independently controlled sources.
