# Perception and the station of 'Alchemist'

Track how a capacity becomes a *claim* and how a claim becomes a *socially recognized station*.

## Questions
- How did craft specialists explain visible transformations?
- Who treated transformation as sacred, philosophical, economic, or technical?
- Who could claim exclusive access? How was authority challenged?
- What did patrons fund? What did rival specialists dispute?
- What words did contemporaries actually use? When does the modern word 'alchemist' become anachronistic?
- Does a text **manifest from** an already recognized station, or help **create** that station by constructing authority?

Keep evidence of formal position distinct from our interpretive model. A workshop expert, temple specialist, philosopher, author, and professional alchemist are not automatically the same social station.
