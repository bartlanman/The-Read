# Practitioners and lineage

Create one prosopographical record per historical figure or securely identifiable authorial community.

## Candidate leads — NOT verified lineage
- Pseudo-Democritus / disputed identifications of the author of the *Four Books*.
- Maria the Jewess / Mary the Jewess (dates and attributions uncertain).
- Zosimos of Panopolis and Theosebeia.
- Late-antique commentators and Byzantine compilers.
- Arabic alchemical authors and corpora, including the disputed Jābirian corpus.
- Latin translators, commentators, and early modern investigators.

## Record structure
- Original-language names, variants, and modern conventional name.
- Earliest attestation; plausible dates; places.
- Practice, attribution, station, patrons, opponents, named teachers/students.
- Writings; direct citations; later ascriptions and reception.
- **Relation type:** documented person-to-person contact / citation / text influence / later legendary association / inference.
- Sources and confidence.

Avoid filling missing historical links with presumed initiatory descent.
