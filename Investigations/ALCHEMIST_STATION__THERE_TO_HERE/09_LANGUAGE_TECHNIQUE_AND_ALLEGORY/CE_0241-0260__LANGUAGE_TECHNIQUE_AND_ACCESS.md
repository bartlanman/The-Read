# Window 23 — Language, Technique, Allegory and Access: When a Performance is Certified but a Recipe Isn't

**Period 241–260 CE.** **Question:** What words, material operations and access systems are actually witnessed in the years under investigation, and which ambiguities arise in later transmission? This is not a claim that mid-third-century Egypt had a universally recognized “Alchemist” profession. [Main Window 23](../02_CHRONOLOGY_20_YEAR_WINDOWS/CE_0241-0260_CE__WINDOW.md) · [primary/scholarly sources](../08_SOURCE_REGISTER/CE_0241-0260__SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

## 1 — Three different kinds of legible material action

**Metal / official naming (248/249):** [RPC VIII 3329](https://rpc.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/coins/8/3329) records a debased-silver tetradrachm whose regnal year `LϚ` is **six**. Here a public material object gives the authority and dating of issuance without identifying its craftspeople. Greek imperial titulature answers **who claims the authority**, not **who mastered production**, still less who produced gold by elemental change.

**Sacrifice / witnessed certification (14 and 17 June 250):** [P.Oxy IV 658](https://papyri.info/ddbdp/p.oxy;4;658) and [P.Mich III 157](https://papyri.info/ddbdp/p.mich;3;157) use formulaic petitions; **two actual manuscripts**, with distinctly named families. They communicate a claim about a **publicly observed performance** and sought endorsement by appointed commissioners. The latter actually names the officials who saw the act. **Authority originates in the state's certification structure**, not the mystery of a hidden operation.

**Alchemical texts / later testimony:** [Dufault 2019](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2ks0g83x) and [Martelli 2022](https://cris.unibo.it/retrieve/e80dea99-da60-40df-a5a5-589724690433/Martelli_2022%20%28Transmuting%20Tinctures%29.pdf) permit comparison with technical recipes, but neither makes Leiden/Stockholm papyri a certain mid-century original nor dates a Maria/Zosimos workshop to 250.

## 2 — A miniature lexicon: original terms with specific documentary function

| Wording | Witness | What it establishes | What it does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| `τοῖς ἐπὶ τῶν θυσιῶν ᾑρημένοις` | P.Mich. III 157 | Recipients *chosen/appointed over sacrifices* | secret masters of transmutation |
| `ἐθύσαμεν` | P.Mich. III 157 | petitioners say **we sacrificed** | laboratory proof or private sincerity |
| `ἐσπείσαμεν` | P.Mich. III 157 | **we poured libations** | chemical distillation |
| `ἐγευσάμεθα` | P.Mich. III 157 | **we tasted** offerings | a pharmacological test |
| `εἴδαμεν ὑμᾶς θυσιάζοντας` | P.Mich. III 157 | **we saw you sacrificing** (named commissioners) | a generalized state training credential |
| `κελευσθέντα` | P.Oxy IV 658 | **what was commanded** | original edict's precise missing wording |
| `LϚ` | RPC VIII 3329 | Egyptian regnal **year 6**, 248/249 | coded recipe ingredients |
| `magoi` / 'Magi from Egypt' | Dionysius quoted in Eusebius HE VII.10 | **polemical hostile representation** of a rival | attested professional `chēmeutai` |
| `chēmeia`, `baphai` | Greek alchemical reception discussed by Dufault | categories of alchemical writing / tincture, coloring | automatic contemporary naming of coin minters or June-250 petitioners |

**Method:** Manuscript readings are in Greek; English glosses here are working translations of bounded phrases, not a claim that modern scholarly nomenclature was the actor's self-description. Never assign unobserved intentions based solely on a word's later associative meaning.

## 3 — Is material transformation the same as changing the appearance of matter?

- **Physical alloy / debasement:** metal remains physically transformed by mixture, working and minting. We have an object, but **no recorded mid-250s formula** for the exact RPC type.
- **Gilding / surface coloring:** alters a surface and can look gold-like; no evidence of a nucleus changing atomic number.
- **Imitative alloys:** may achieve economic or visual substitution; again **not elemental transmutation**.
- **Dyeing / tinting:** changes optical characteristics of textiles, stones or metallic surfaces; a technically competent process can be reproducible and valuable even without the promised ontology of an alchemical text.
- **Elemental conversion:** requires evidence that a base element actually became a different element; not supported by the papyri or coin. Modern nuclear transformation does not retroactively validate ancient chrysopoeia.
- **Scholarly horizon:** Leiden X and Stockholm (late 3rd / 4th c physical witnesses) preserve dyeing and imitative metal recipes. [Martelli 2022] [Ferrari et al. 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352409X20305332) provide examples of making a silver-like appearance with alum-treated copper scale. **Their experiments test technical mechanism in modern laboratories, not exact execution in 250 CE**.

## 4 — Secrecy audit, not a romantic explanation

| Rival account of opacity | Evidence in 241–260 | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **State compulsion / ritual eligibility** | original June 250 certificates + officials who endorse | **ATTESTED** for religion |
| **Persecution avoidance** | Cyprian Ep.80; Dionysius via Eusebius 7.11 | **ATTESTED/RETROSPECTIVE** Christian pressure; **NO SECURE ALCHEMICAL LINK** |
| **Workshop trade secret / protected economic knowledge** | no securely local and dated alchemist's confidential recipe or oath identified | **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** |
| **Knowledge requiring initiation, practice or apparatus** | later recipes are operational and materially specific; trainable skill reasonable | **SOURCE-SUPPORTED GENERAL COMPARISON**, no credential in window |
| **Danger of reagents** | potentially risky processes in later transmitted recipe traditions | **NO SECURE EVIDENCE** concealment to protect against toxicity |
| **Prestige and learned playful code** | Africanus's lost appendix and geometric/music notation, composition 222–235 | **INHERITED CROSS-WINDOW**, not 250 incident |
| **Loss of textual key or language context** | missing Africanus appendix; later manuscripts for Maria/Zosimos | **ATTESTED COPY-CHAIN LOSS**, no proof of deliberate concealment |
| **Demonological/political slander** | Dionysius's Egyptian Magi accusation reported by Eusebius | **RETROSPECTIVE POLEMIC** with unproven chemical referent |

**Safety / capacity distinction:** Potentially dangerous materials do not prove that their hazards motivated secrecy. In this particular document cluster, where officials are explicitly described, the words define a **public legal demonstration** rather than concealed elite chemistry.

## 5 — The strongest causal non-equivalence

A direct pressure on a **Christian bishop** is not thereby direct pressure on **religious secrecy generally**; an account blaming Egyptian **Magi** is not direct evidence that **alchemical writers** were responsible for persecution; the imperial administration's control of **ritual** does not create an alchemical certification scheme. These are different institutions, actors and evidentiary records. The analogical comparison becomes valid only by labeling it **COMPARISON, NOT LINEAGE**.

**The recurrent historical mistake:** a source says a ritual is certified → historian says an ancient institution controlled secret material techniques → later story calls a suspect person a magus → we conclude “the persecuted Alchemist hid chemical secrets.” Each arrow is missing a documentary bridge. None may be silently supplied.

## 6 — The Outside: how certification and naming change authority

**248/249:** the mint publicly declares monetary authority through official metal, but the competence of artisans remains institutionally anonymous.

**June 250:** official documents create a legible religious *eligibility verdict* by certifying a bodily performance. Public authorization is not inferred; it is literally described in an original dated record. Public ritual may coexist with private beliefs; the documents cannot measure private conviction.

**257–258:** imperial sanctions put pressure on Christian station and force a choice between maintaining communal worship and obeying governors; Dionysius's report describes exile from his office's place. This is evidenced at the actor level (Christian bishop). It says nothing securely about whether an unnamed metallurgist changed techniques, hid manuscripts or fled.

**260/after:** Eusebius reproduces in Greek a rescript ascribed to Gallienus restoring worship-site access; **do not force it to pre-261 if timing remains open**.

**Later reception:** Zosimos (around 300) can cite older authorities (Maria, Pseudo-Democritus) and distinguish his position against rivals. The combination of reader, writer, audience and patron is potentially the relevant future alchemical station **but must be dated on its own evidence**.

## 7 — THERE → HERE, and the surviving problem

**THERE:** a world of actual skilled metalworking, written technique, public state inscription, ritual offices and privately transmitted expertise.

**ENCOUNTER:** a specifically attested 250 religious duty becomes a witnessed certificate; an issued official coin demonstrates another material form of authority; religious conflict produces named clerical proceedings.

**HERE:** the *historian* can now compare **actual material work**, **public proof**, **administrative certification**, **social reputation** and **textual authority** without calling them the same station. This is a methodological clarification, **not** a demonstrated founding of the Alchemist in 241–260.

**REMAINDER:** absence of dated 250 workshop recipe; unknown actual motives for guarded material knowledge; chronological uncertainty of Maria; no proven encounter between Magi polemic and alchemical material practice; unknown reasons for recipe-key disappearance; unverified precise timing of Gallienus restitution.

**NEXT SOURCE TEST:** In 261–280, find original documentation linking **one named person** with both **specific performed material operation** and **recognized authority to interpret or certify transformation**. If none, retain the failure-to-find and compare later Zosimos without assigning him fictional earlier decades.
