# Window 09 — When one station crosses linguistic or institutional boundaries

## A. Three scripts, different authorities: Philae, 29 BCE
Gallus's victory inscription is carved in Latin, Greek and Egyptian hieroglyphs. The Egyptian content is **not** exactly the Latin/Greek text translated word-for-word. The new prefect legitimates a Roman office inside an Egyptian monumental convention. This is a documented **change in public framing**, not chemical encryption.

Question: Does a new station require adapting how it describes itself so that its authority is recognizable by different audiences?

## B. Ancient priestly knowledge and secrecy as a later account: Strabo's two passages
- **17.1.8:** Ptolemaic kings appointed the Mouseion priest; Caesar/Augustus now does. **Same role, different authority**, as reported.
- **17.1.29:** Strabo recalls Heliopolis learned priests missing in his day and a much earlier Plato/Eudoxus story about priests who were `μυστικούς`, `δυσμεταδότους`, and `ἀπεκρύψαντο` — terms about withholding and reluctance to teach astronomy. Those statements belong to the **reported earlier story**, not an observation of contemporary technical censorship.
- This source **does not attest hidden transmutation chemistry**. It does attest a first-century CE Greek scholarly discourse about guarded Egyptian knowledge.

## C. Knowledge categorized, not proven: Han court 26 BCE
- `校中祕書` = collating internal/imperial writings, not automatically secret formulas or an initiation test.
- `方技` = a class of arts/technical and medical writings supervised in the collation scheme by *physician Li Zhuguo*, **not** Liu Xiang.
- Liu Xiang's assignment to classics and philosophy shows that **a person's later documentary skill can become institutionally recognized even after earlier technical failure**.

## Three distinct thresholds that must not be merged
1. **Authority threshold:** who can confer an office? (King → Augustus at Mouseion).
2. **Language threshold:** how is legitimacy presented across audiences? (Gallus's different-script texts).
3. **Competence threshold:** who is entrusted to classify texts versus carry out procedures? (Liu Xiang vs Li Zhuguo).

**Working claim:** A book, monument or office can cross a political boundary while the ability to perform whatever technique it names does not necessarily follow.
