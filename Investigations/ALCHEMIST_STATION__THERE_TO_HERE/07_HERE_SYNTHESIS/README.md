# HERE — Synthesis and warranted thresholds

This is not a predetermined origin myth.

For each completed tranche of windows, extract:
- What the previous world permitted.
- The new encounter, pressure, institution, or technique.
- New perception and who expressed it.
- The specific evidence that a station emerged, shifted, or dissolved.
- The new kind of text or social relationship that became possible.
- What persisted unchanged and what failed to transmit.
- Explicit unresolved remainders and counterevidence.

**Provisional guiding distinction:** changing matter ≠ explaining matter ≠ claiming authority over change ≠ being recognized by a community as an Alchemist.

Never convert a suggestive pattern into a documented historical cause without primary and secondary evidence.
